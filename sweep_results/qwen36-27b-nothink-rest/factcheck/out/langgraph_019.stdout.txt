Verdict: NOT_SUPPORTED
Rationale: The claim states that the single was released in 1998. While the evidence confirms that the song 'Believe' was written by Brian Higgins and released as a single in 1998, it also establishes that 'The Very Best of Cher' is a compilation album released on April 1, 2003. The claim implies the single itself was released in 1998 *as part of* or *in the context of* the album 'The Very Best of Cher', or conflates the release date of the single with the album. More critically, the phrasing 'This single... included in the album The Very Best of Cher was released in 1998' attributes the 1998 release date to the entity defined by its inclusion in the 2003 album. Since the album 'The Very Best of Cher' was not released until 2003, the statement that the single (in the context of this album) was released in 1998 is factually inconsistent with the album's release date. Furthermore, 'Believe' was originally included in the studio album 'Believe' (1998), not 'The Very Best of Cher' (2003). The claim incorrectly associates the 1998 release with the 2003 compilation.
