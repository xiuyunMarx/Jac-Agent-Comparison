Photo: Cl\u00e9ment Morin\n\nHan Kang  \nNobel Prize in Literature 2024\n\nBorn: 27 November 1970, Gwangju, South Korea\n\nResidence at the time of the award: Seoul, South Korea\n\nPrize motivation: \u201cfor her intense poetic prose that confronts historical\ntraumas and exposes the fragility of human life\u201d\n\nLanguage: Korean\n\nPrize share: 1/1\n\n###  Work\n\nHan Kang began her career in 1993 as a poet, but has since written mainly\nnovels and short stories. In her oeuvre, she confronts historical traumas and\ninvisible sets of rules and, in each of her works, exposes the fragility of\nhuman life. She has a unique awareness of the connections between body and\nsoul, the living and the dead, and in her poetic and experimental style has\nbecome an innovator in contemporary prose. Among her works are The Vegetarian,\nHuman Acts and We Do Not Part.\n\nTo cite this section  \nMLA style: Han Kang - Facts - 2024\\. NobelPrize.org. Nobel Prize Outreach\n2026.
