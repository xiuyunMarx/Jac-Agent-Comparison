[48\ncomments](https://www.goodreads.com/blog/show/2879-meet-the-winners-of-\nthe-2024-goodreads-choice-awards#top)\n\nShare\n\n[![](https://images.gr-\nassets.com/misc/1731606727-1731606727_goodreads_misc.png)](https://www.goodreads.com/choiceawards/best-\nbooks-2024)\n\nThe 16th Annual Goodreads Choice Awards are now official! With a new record of\nmore than 6.2 million votes cast, the 2024 awards continue the Goodreads\ntradition of honoring readers\u2019 favorite books of the year.  \n  \nThis time around, we\u2019ve got 15 separate categories with 300 nominated books\ntotal. You can find the winners in each listed below, along with a link to all\nthe nominated books in that category.  \n  \nAlert readers will note that several returning winners are featured this year.\n[Emily Henry](https://www.goodreads.com/author/show/13905555.Emily_Henry\n\"https://www.goodreads.com/author/show/13905555.Emily_Henry\") scored her\nfourth Readers\u2019 Favorite Romance win in a row with  _[Funny\nStory](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/194803835-funny-story\n\"https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/194803835-funny-story\")_ (soon to be a\nmovie, [apparently](https://variety.com/2024/film/news/funny-story-movie-\nemily-henry-writing-1236063486/ \"https://variety.com/2024/film/news/funny-\nstory-movie-emily-henry-writing-1236063486/\")). [Stephen\nKing](https://www.goodreads.com/author/show/3389.Stephen_King\n\"https://www.goodreads.com/author/show/3389.Stephen_King\")\u2019s short fiction\ncollection  _[You Like It\nDarker](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/201242757-you-like-it-darker\n\"https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/201242757-you-like-it-darker\")_ took the\nHorror prize, giving King a total of 11 career wins across four categories\nover the years.  \n  \nOther returning winners include [Kristin\nHannah](https://www.goodreads.com/author/show/54493.Kristin_Hannah\n\"https://www.goodreads.com/author/show/54493.Kristin_Hannah\"), [T.J.\nKlune](https://www.goodreads.com/author/show/5073330.T_J_Klune\n\"https://www.goodreads.com/author/show/5073330.T_J_Klune\"), [Sarah J.\nMaas](https://www.goodreads.com/author/show/3433047.Sarah_J_Maas\n\"https://www.goodreads.com/author/show/3433047.Sarah_J_Maas\"), [Alice\nOseman](https://www.goodreads.com/author/show/7789423.Alice_Oseman\n\"https://www.goodreads.com/author/show/7789423.Alice_Oseman\"), and [Rebecca\nRoss](https://www.goodreads.com/author/show/14926516.Rebecca_Ross\n\"https://www.goodreads.com/author/show/14926516.Rebecca_Ross\"). We had a few\nlandslide victories this year, including Hannah\u2019s historical fiction novel\n_[The Women](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/127305853-the-women\n\"https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/127305853-the-women\")_ , [Kelly\nBishop](https://www.goodreads.com/author/show/32988350.Kelly_Bishop\n\"https://www.goodreads.com/author/show/32988350.Kelly_Bishop\")\u2019s memoir,\n_[The Third Gilmore Girl](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/207298106-the-\nthird-gilmore-girl \"https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/207298106-the-third-\ngilmore-girl\")_ , and [Kaliane\nBradley](https://www.goodreads.com/author/show/22032713.Kaliane_Bradley\n\"https://www.goodreads.com/author/show/22032713.Kaliane_Bradley\")\u2019s debut sci-\nfi adventure,  _[The Ministry of\nTime](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/199798179-the-ministry-of-time\n\"https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/199798179-the-ministry-of-time\")_.  \n  \nCongratulations to all the nominees\u2014thank you most sincerely for giving us\nanother year of great reading. Thanks, too, to the global Goodreads community\nand everyone who voted. Readers\nlove Espach\u2019s twisty tonal shifts\u2014from high drama to screwball comedy of\nmanners\u2014and the ultimate moral of her story: Go with the flow; who knows what\nmight happen?  \n  \n**[See all of this year\u2019s Fiction nominees\nhere.](https://www.goodreads.com/choiceawards/readers-favorite-fiction-\nbooks-2024)**\n\n  \n  \n\n[ ![The Women](https://i.gr-\nassets.com/images/S/compressed.photo.goodreads.com/books/1733493019l/127305853._SY475_.jpg)\n](/book/show/127305853-the-women)\n\nWant to Read saving\u2026\n\n**[Readers\u2019 Favorite Historical\nFiction](https://www.goodreads.com/choiceawards/readers-favorite-historical-\nfiction-books-2024): _[The\nWomen](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/127305853-the-women)_**  \nby [Kristin\nHannah](https://www.goodreads.com/author/show/54493.Kristin_Hannah)\n\nAn insightful meditation on courage, character, and women gone to war, Kristin\nHannah\u2019s novel introduces idealistic Army nurse Frances \u201cFrankie\u201d McGrath as\nshe volunteers in Vietnam, circa 1965. Hannah has two\nGCA awards on her shelf already, for 2015\u2019s _The Nightingale_ and 2018\u2019s _The\nGreat Alone._  \n  \n**[See all of this year\u2019s Historical Fiction nominees\nhere.](https://www.goodreads.com/choiceawards/readers-favorite-historical-\nfiction-books-2024)**\n\n  \n  \n\n[ ![The God of the Woods](https://i.gr-\nassets.com/images/S/compressed.photo.goodreads.com/books/1717970585l/199700434._SY475_.jpg)\n](/book/show/199700434-the-god-of-the-woods)\n\nWant to Read saving\u2026\n\n**[Readers\u2019 Favorite Mystery&\nThriller](https://www.goodreads.com/choiceawards/readers-favorite-mystery-\nthriller-books-2024): _[The God of the\nWoods](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/199700434-the-god-of-the-woods)_**  \nby [Liz Moore](https://www.goodreads.com/author/show/5435162.Liz_Moore)\n\nIn 1975, troubled teenager Barbara Van Laar vanished from her bunk at the\nsummer camp owned by her wealthy family. Making things so much worse: Her\nolder brother disappeared much the same way, 16 years ago.
