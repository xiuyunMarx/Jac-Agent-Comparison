Yoon**\n\n**Empower! Be YONSEI!**\n\nGreetings!  Yonsei University has always followed the righteous path through\nthe twists and turns of Korea\u2019s modern history. As a distinguished global\nacademic institution, Yonsei has made significant contributions to society and\nhumanity. Inheriting this prestigious tradition, we aim to advance into a new\nera of Yonsei, thereby opening the gateway to the future with what makes us\nuniquely YONSEI.  \n  \nIn the age of \u201cBig Blur,\" we are experiencing the collapse of boundaries\nbetween academia and industry. We are confronted with the dual challenges of\ndigital transformation and energy transition on the fiercely competitive\nbattlefield of innovation. Words like creativity, consilience, and convergence\nhave long since become keywords in our society. Innovation is the only way to\nstay relevant in this rapidly changing international environment.  \n  \nYonsei's enduring mission for over 140 years has been to nurture talents who\ndrive innovation and change. We will continue to build on this foundation, to\ncultivate individuals who proactively tackle the problems of global society\nwhile pursuing the values of coexistence. We will strengthen programs that\nfoster creative future talent, such as the \u201cStudent-Designed Semester,\u201d\nenabling students to freely explore academia, to grow as independent agents of\nthe future society.  \n  \nYonsei\u2019s key asset is the optimal campus in which we can navigate diverse\nacademic disciplines. We aim to bring together Yonsei\u2019s academic capabilities\nto address: the meaning and quality of a healthy life in an aging society;\nredefine human dignity and ethics in the era of artificial intelligence;\nimplement ecological practices to confront climate crises; and consolidate\nefforts to solve international conflicts and societal challenges. This\ninterdisciplinary research initiative is rooted in a humanistic understanding\nof humanity and nature embedded in Yonsei spirit. With a focus on future\nsustainability, including artificial intelligence, biotechnology, and\nrenewable energy, Yonsei will seek to elevate our global research\ncompetitiveness.  \n  \nThe new Yonsei era will be realized through \"Empowering Yonsei,\" founded on\nthe principles of \"governance centered on respect and communication.\"\nEnhancing the pride of the Yonsei community through the autonomous and\nresponsible operation of each unit is the first step towards being Yonsei.\nEnsuring a happy campus, where all Yonseians can pursue studies, research, and\npersonal growth to their hearts\u2019 content, will indeed be the shortcut for\nYonsei to leap forward into a future beyond boundaries. Please join us on this\njourney.  \n  \nThank you.\n\n20th President of Yonsei University\n\nDong-Sup Yoon\n\n## Biography\n\n### Education\n\n  * 1981.03 - 1987.02 M.D. Yonsei University College of Medicine\n  * 1995.09 - 1997.08 M.S.
