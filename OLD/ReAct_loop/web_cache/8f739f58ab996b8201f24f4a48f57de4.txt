At just\n40, Liang joined the billionaires\u2019 club with a $1 billion fortune, thanks to\ndeveloping one of the world\u2019s most cost-efficient AI models. [Zhang Yiming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhang_Yiming) \u2013 $65.5\nBillion\n\n**Source of Wealth:** Internet Media | **Residence:** Singapore  \nThe **ByteDance** founder tops the Richest Chinese Billionaires 2025 list.\nDespite uncertainty over TikTok in the U.S., ByteDance\u2019s valuation soared to\n$312 billion. Zhang\u2019s fortune grew more than 50% in a year, supported by AI\nbreakthroughs such as Doubao, the chatbot with 82 million monthly users.\n\nADVERTISEMENT\n\n### 2\\. [Zhong Shanshan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhong_Shanshan) \u2013 $57.7\nBillion\n\n**Source of Wealth:** Beverages, Pharmaceuticals | **Residence:** Hangzhou  \nThe **Nongfu Spring** founder lost his four-year reign at the top but remains\nimmensely wealthy. Sales growth slowed after a consumer backlash, yet Zhong\nstill controls China\u2019s leading bottled water brand and a pharmaceuticals\nempire.\n\n### 3\\. [Ma Huateng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ma_Huateng) \u2013 $56.2 Billion\n\n**Source of Wealth:** Online Games | **Residence:** Shenzhen  \nTencent\u2019s shares nearly doubled in 2024, driving Ma\u2019s wealth to new highs.\nTencent expanded into AI and gaming partnerships, investing in Ubisoft\nfranchises while integrating AI into WeChat and launching the Yuanbao digital\nassistant.\n\n### 4\\. [Lei Jun ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lei_Jun)\u2013 $43.5 Billion\n\n**Source of Wealth:** Smartphones, Automobiles | **Residence:** Beijing  \nXiaomi\u2019s founder saw a dramatic surge in wealth, driven by strong demand for\nthe SU7 electric sedan. Despite a recent fatal crash under driver-assistance\nmode, Xiaomi raised billions to expand EV production.\n\nADVERTISEMENT\n\n### 5\\. [Colin Huang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colin_Huang) \u2013 $42.3\nBillion\n\n**Source of Wealth:** E-Commerce | **Residence:** Shanghai  \nPDD Holdings\u2019 creator slipped in the rankings as competition and U.S. tariff\nthreats challenge Temu\u2019s global expansion. Still, sales reached $54 billion in\n2024, keeping Huang among the elite.\n\n### 6\\. [William Ding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ding_Lei) \u2013 $33.3 Billion\n\n**Source of Wealth:** Online Games | **Residence:** Hangzhou  \nNetEase\u2019s growth slowed, but Ding remains influential. Marvel Rivals, a\ncollaboration with Marvel Games, is expected to boost revenues in 2025 after\nmodest performance in 2024.\n\n### 7\\. [Jack Ma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Ma) \u2013 $28.6 Billion\n\n**Source of Wealth:** E-Commerce | **Residence:** Hangzhou  \nThe **Alibaba** cofounder returned to prominence after meeting with President\nXi Jinping. Alibaba is investing $53 billion into AI infrastructure, aiming to\ncement its position as a global tech leader.\n\n### 8\\. [He Xiangjian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/He_Xiangjian) \u2013 $27\nBillion\n\n**Source of Wealth:** Home Appliances | **Residence:** Foshan  \nThe **Midea Group** cofounder continues to benefit from China\u2019s consumer\nsubsidy programs. The company raised $4 billion in a share sale to fund\ninternational expansion and supply chain upgrades.\n\n### 9\\. [Wang Chuanfu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wang_Chuanfu) \u2013 $26.4\nBillion\n\n**Source of Wealth:** Automobiles | **Residence:** Shenzhen  \nThe **BYD** chairman overtook Tesla in global EV sales in 2024. BYD shipped\n4.27 million cars worldwide and aims for 5.5 million in 2025, underscoring\nChina\u2019s dominance in electric mobility.\n\n### 10\\. [Lu Xiangyang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lu_Xiangyang) \u2013 $20\nBillion\n\n**Source of Wealth:** Automobiles | **Residence:** Guangzhou  \nLu, cousin of Wang Chuanfu, co-founded **BYD** in 1995 and is now vice\nchairman. His fortune comes from BYD shares and investments in lithium mining\nthrough Youngy Investment Holding Group.\n\n* * *\n\n## Conclusion\n\nThe **Richest Chinese Billionaires 2025** list underscores the shifting\ndynamics of China\u2019s wealth. AI pioneers, EV visionaries, and e-commerce\ninnovators now dominate the rankings. Zhang Yiming\u2019s rise reflects the global\nappetite for Chinese tech, while Zhong Shanshan\u2019s decline shows the volatility\nof consumer markets.
