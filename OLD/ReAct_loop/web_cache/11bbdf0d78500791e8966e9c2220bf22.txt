He became the president of his family's real estate business in 1971, renamed it\nthe Trump Organization\n, and began acquiring and building skyscrapers, hotels, casinos, and golf courses. He launched side ventures, many licensing the Trump name, and filed for six business bankruptcies in the 1990s and 2000s. From 2004 to 2015, he hosted the reality television show\nThe Apprentice\n, bolstering his reputation as a wealthy tycoon. Presenting himself as a political outsider, Trump won the\n2016 presidential election\nagainst\nDemocratic Party\nnominee\nHillary Clinton\n.\nDuring\nhis first presidency\n, Trump imposed\na travel ban\non seven\nMuslim-majority countries\n, expanded the\nMexico\u2013United States border wall\n, and enforced\na family separation policy\non the border.\nHe rolled back environmental and business regulations, signed the\nTax Cuts and Jobs Act\n, and appointed three Supreme Court justices. He withdrew the U.S. from agreements on climate and trade and started\na trade war with China\n. In response to the\nCOVID-19 pandemic\nin 2020, he downplayed its severity, contradicted health officials, and signed the bipartisan\nCARES Act\n. After losing the\n2020 presidential election\nto\nJoe Biden\n, Trump refused to concede defeat and\nattempted to overturn the result\n, resulting in the\nJanuary\n6 Capitol attack\nin 2021. He was impeached twice\n\u2014\nin 2019\nfor abuse of power and obstruction of Congress and\nin 2021\nfor incitement of insurrection\n\u2014\nand acquitted by the Senate both times.\nIn 2023, Trump was found liable in New York state civil cases\nfor sexual abuse and defamation\nand\nfor business fraud\n. In May 2024, he was found guilty in a New York state court on 34 counts\nof falsifying business records\n, making him the first U.S. president convicted of a felony. After winning the\n2024 presidential election\nagainst Vice President\nKamala Harris\n, he was given a no-penalty sentence, and two federal felony indictments for\nretention of classified documents\nand\nobstruction of the 2020 election\nwere dismissed\nwithout prejudice\n.\nTrump began\nhis second presidency\nby initiating\nmass layoffs of federal workers\n. He\nimposed tariffs\non nearly all countries at the highest level since the\nGreat Depression\nand signed the\nOne Big Beautiful Bill Act\n.
