Justin Timberlake\ntook fifth.\n\n## Do we know about the top songs?\n\nKendrick Lamar's Not Like Us set the internet alight. The Drake diss track\nsparked days of rap beef, five Grammy nominations and the top-searched song of\n2024.\n\nK-pop star Rose and Bruno Mar's collab APT. came second. The lowest spot on\nthe podium was taken by Bling-Bang-Bang-Born from Creepy Nuts.\n\nYouTuber-turned-boxer-turned-rapper KSI snagged the fourth most-searched song\nwith Thick of It.\n\nObviously, the inescapable and catchy Espresso by Sabrina Carpenter got fifth.\n\n## What about the top TV shows?\n\nNetflix's Baby Reindeer was certain to take the top spot with his haunting\ndepiction of sexual assault and trauma.
