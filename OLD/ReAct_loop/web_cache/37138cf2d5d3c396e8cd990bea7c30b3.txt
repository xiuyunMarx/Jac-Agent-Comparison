For other uses, see\nTaylor Swift (disambiguation)\n.\nTaylor Swift\nSwift in 2023\nBorn\nTaylor Alison Swift\n(\n1989-12-13\n)\nDecember 13, 1989\n(age\n36)\nWest Reading, Pennsylvania\n, US\nOccupation\nSinger-songwriter\nYears\nactive\n2003\u2013present\nOrganization\nTaylor Swift Productions\nWorks\nAlbums\nsingles\nsongs\nvideos\nperformances\nSpouse\nTravis Kelce\n\u200b\n(\nm.\n2026\n)\n\u200b\nRelatives\nAustin Swift\n(brother)\nMarjorie Finlay\n(grandmother)\nAwards\nFull list\nMusical career\nOrigin\nNashville, Tennessee\nGenres\nPop\ncountry\nfolk\nInstruments\nVocals\nguitar\npiano\nbanjo\nukulele\nLabels\nBig Machine\nRepublic\nWebsite\ntaylorswift\n.com\nSignature\nTaylor Alison Swift\n(born December 13, 1989) is an American singer-songwriter\n.\nAn influential figure in popular culture\n, Swift is known for her autobiographical songwriting and artistic reinventions. She is the\nhighest-grossing live music artist\n, the\nwealthiest female musician\n, and one of the\nbest-selling music artists of all time\n.\nSwift signed with\nBig Machine Records\nin 2005 and debuted as a\ncountry\nsinger with the albums\nTaylor Swift\n(2006) and\nFearless\n(2008). The singles \"\nTeardrops on My Guitar\n\", \"\nLove Story\n\", and \"\nYou Belong with Me\n\" found\ncrossover\nsuccess on\ncountry\nand\npop radio\nformats. Her songs began incorporating stronger\nrock\nelements with\nSpeak Now\n(2010) and\nelectronic\nstyles with\nRed\n(2012). She subsequently recalibrated her artistic identity from country to\npop\nwith the\nsynth-pop\nalbum\n1989\n(2014), while ensuing media scrutiny inspired the\ntrap\n-imbued\nReputation\n(2017). Through the 2010s, Swift accumulated the US\nBillboard\nHot 100\nnumber-one singles \"\nWe Are Never Ever Getting Back Together\n\", \"\nShake It Off\n\", \"\nBlank Space\n\", \"\nBad Blood\n\", and \"\nLook What You Made Me Do\n\".\nShifting to\nRepublic Records\nin 2018, Swift released the\neclectic\npop album\nLover\n(2019) and\nre-recorded\nfour of her first six albums due to\na dispute with Big Machine\n. She explored\nindie folk\non the 2020 albums\nFolklore\nand\nEvermore\n, followed by mellower pop subgenres on\nMidnights\n(2022) and\nThe Tortured Poets Department\n(2024), as well as\nsoft rock\non\nThe Life of a Showgirl\n(2025). The singles \"\nCardigan\n\", \"\nWillow\n\", \"\nAll Too Well (Taylor's Version)\n\", \"\nAnti-Hero\n\", \"\nCruel Summer\n\", \"\nIs It Over Now?\n\", \"\nFortnight\n\", \"\nThe Fate of Ophelia\n\", \"\nOpalite\n\", and \"\nI Knew It, I Knew You\n\" topped the Hot 100. Her\nEras Tour\n(2023\u20132024) and its associated film,\nTaylor Swift: The Eras Tour\n(2023), are the\nhighest-grossing concert tour\nand\nconcert film of all time\n.\nSwift is the only artist to have been named the\nIFPI Global Recording Artist of the Year\nsix times. A record eight of her albums have each sold over a million copies first-week in the US. Publications such as\nRolling Stone\nand\nBillboard\nhave ranked Swift among the greatest artists of all time. She is the first individual from\nthe arts\nto be named\nTime\nPerson of the Year\n(2023) and the youngest female inductee into the\nSongwriters Hall of Fame\n(2026).\nHer accolades\ninclude 14\nGrammy Awards\n\u2014including a record four\nAlbum of the Year\nwins\u2014and a\nPrimetime Emmy Award\n. Swift is the most-awarded artist of the\nAmerican Music Awards\n, the\nBillboard\nMusic Awards\n, and the\nMTV Video Music Awards\n. they are paternal great-great-grandchildren of\nCharles Carmine Antonio Baldi\n, a prominent Philadelphia businessman.\n[\na\n]\nTheir maternal grandmother,\nMarjorie Finlay\n(\nn\u00e9e\nMoehlenkamp\n), was an opera singer whose singing in church became one of Swift's earliest memories of music.\n[\n9\n]\nDuring childhood, Swift spent her\nholiday seasons\non a\nChristmas tree farm\nin Pennsylvania,\n[\n10\n]\nand summers at her family's vacation home in\nStone Harbor, New Jersey\n, where she occasionally performed acoustic songs at a local coffee shop.\n[\n11\n]\nRaised Christian,\n[\n12\n]\nshe attended preschool and kindergarten at a\nMontessori\nschool run by the\nBernardine Sisters of St. Francis\nbefore transferring to the Wyndcroft School in\nPottstown\n.\n[\n13\n]\n[\n14\n]\nWhen her family moved to\nWyomissing\n, she attended\nWyomissing Area Junior/Senior High School\n.\n[\n15\n]\n[\n16\n]\nAt age nine, she aspired to a career in\nmusical theater\n, performing at local festivals and in Berks Youth Theatre Academy productions,\n[\n17\n]\n[\n18\n]\nand traveling regularly to New York City for vocal and acting lessons.\n[\n19\n]\n[\n20\n]\nAfter watching a documentary about\nFaith Hill\n, she changed her goal and became determined to pursue a\ncountry music\ncareer in\nNashville, Tennessee\n.\n[\n21\n]\nAt the age of 11, Swift traveled to Nashville with her mother to visit record labels and submit\ndemo\ntapes of\nDolly Parton\nand\nDixie Chicks\nkaraoke covers.\n[\n22\n]\nShe was rejected by all the labels, which led her to focus on songwriting.\n[\n23\n]\nShe started learning the guitar at the age of 12 with the help of a computer repairman and local musician who assisted Swift in writing an original song.\n[\n24\n]\nIn 2003, she and her parents started working with the talent manager Dan Dymtrow.
