**bold**.[2]\n\n### Journalism\n\n[[edit](/w/index.php?title=2024_Pulitzer_Prize&action=edit&section=2 \"Edit\nsection: Journalism\")]\n\n[Public\nService](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pulitzer_Prize_for_Public_Service\n\"Pulitzer Prize for Public Service\")  \n---  \n_**[ProPublica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ProPublica \"ProPublica\")**_ ,\nfor the work of Joshua Kaplan, Justin Elliott, [Brett\nMurphy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brett_Murphy \"Brett Murphy\"), Alex\nMierjeski and Kirsten Berg, for \"groundbreaking and ambitious reporting that\npierced the thick wall of secrecy surrounding the [Supreme\nCourt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supreme_Court_of_United_States \"Supreme\nCourt of United States\") to reveal how a small group of politically\ninfluential billionaires wooed justices with lavish gifts and travel, pushing\nthe Court to adopt its first code of conduct.\"  \n_[KFF Health News](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KFF_Health_News \"KFF Health\nNews\") and [Cox Media Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cox_Media_Group\n\"Cox Media Group\")_, \"for uncovering millions of cases in which the Social\nSecurity Administration overpaid beneficiaries, then demanded immediate\nrepayment \u2014 imposing debts on elderly and disabled people who had already\nspent the funds.\"  \n_[The Washington Post](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Washington_Post \"The\nWashington Post\")_ , \"for its sobering examination of the AR-15 semi-automatic\nrifle, which forced readers to reckon with the horrors wrought by the weapon\noften used for mass shootings in America.\"  \n[Breaking News\nReporting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pulitzer_Prize_for_Breaking_News_Reporting\n\"Pulitzer Prize for Breaking News Reporting\")  \n---  \n**Staff of _[Lookout Santa\nCruz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lookout_Santa_Cruz \"Lookout Santa Cruz\")_\n, California**, \"for its detailed and nimble community-focused coverage, over\na holiday weekend, of catastrophic flooding and mudslides that displaced\nthousands of residents and destroyed more than 1,000 homes and businesses.\"  \nStaff of the _[Honolulu Civil\nBeat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honolulu_Civil_Beat \"Honolulu Civil\nBeat\")_ , \"for its distinctive, sweeping and urgent coverage of [the Maui\nwildfires](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Hawaii_wildfires \"2023 Hawaii\nwildfires\") that killed more than 100 people and left a historic town in\nruins, reporting that held officials to account and chronicled the aftermath\nand efforts to rebuild.\"  \nStaff of the _[Los Angeles\nTimes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles_Times \"Los Angeles Times\")_ ,\n\"for urgent and thoughtful coverage of a Lunar New Year [overnight\nshooting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Monterey_Park_shooting \"2023\nMonterey Park shooting\") that left 11 senior citizens dead, demonstrating\nclear knowledge of and commitment to the local Asian communities.\"  \n[Investigative\nReporting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pulitzer_Prize_for_Investigative_Reporting\n\"Pulitzer Prize for Investigative Reporting\")  \n---  \n**[Hannah Dreier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannah_Dreier \"Hannah Dreier\")\nof _[The New York Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_New_York_Times \"The\nNew York Times\")_** , \"for a deeply reported series of stories revealing the\nstunning reach of migrant child labor across the United States\u2014and the\ncorporate and governmental failures that perpetuate it.\"  \nCasey Ross and Bob Herman of\n_[STAT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stat_\\(website\\) \"Stat \\(website\\)\")_ ,\n\"for exposing how [UnitedHealth\nGroup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UnitedHealth_Group \"UnitedHealth Group\"),\nthe nation's largest health insurer, used an unregulated algorithm to override\nclinicians' judgments and deny care, highlighting the dangers of AI use in\nmedicine.\"  \nStaff of _[Bloomberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bloomberg_News \"Bloomberg\nNews\")_ , \"for a deep and rigorous investigation of how the US government\naided the global spread of gun violence, prompting the\n[Biden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_Biden \"Joe Biden\") administration to\nhalt most gun exports for 90 days while it reviewed the federal government's\nmarketing relationship with gun manufacturers.\"  \n[Explanatory\nReporting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pulitzer_Prize_for_Explanatory_Reporting\n\"Pulitzer Prize for Explanatory Reporting\")  \n---  \n**[Sarah Stillman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarah_Stillman \"Sarah\nStillman\") of _[The New Yorker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_New_Yorker\n\"The New Yorker\")_** , \"for a searing indictment of our legal system\u2019s\nreliance on the felony murder charge and its disparate consequences, often\ndevastating for communities of color.\"  \nStaff of _Bloomberg_ , \"for rigorous, far-reaching reporting that holds\ncorporate water profiteers to account and exposes how they willfully\nexacerbate the effects of climate change at the expense of less powerful\ncommunities.\"  \nStaffs of _[The Texas Tribune](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Texas_Tribune\n\"The Texas Tribune\")_ , _ProPublica_ , and\n_[FRONTLINE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frontline_\\(American_TV_program\\)\n\"Frontline \\(American TV program\\)\")_ , \"for advancing understanding of law\nenforcement\u2019s catastrophic response to [the mass shooting at a\nUvalde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uvalde_school_shooting \"Uvalde school\nshooting\"), Texas elementary school and also for documenting the political and\npolicy shortcomings that have led to similar deadly police failures across the\ncountry.\"  \n[Local\nReporting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pulitzer_Prize_for_Local_Reporting\n\"Pulitzer Prize for Local Reporting\")  \n---  \n**[Sarah Conway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarah_Conway \"Sarah Conway\") of\n_[City Bureau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_Bureau \"City Bureau\")_ and\nTrina Reynolds-Tyler of the _[Invisible\nInstitute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invisible_Institute \"Invisible\nInstitute\")_** , \"for their investigative series on missing Black girls and\nwomen in Chicago that revealed how systemic racism and police department\nneglect contributed to the crisis.\"  \nJerry Mitchell, Ilyssa Daly, Brian Howey and Nate Rosenfield of _[Mississippi\nToday](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mississippi_Today \"Mississippi Today\")_\nand _[The New York Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_New_York_Times\n\"The New York Times\")_ , \"for their detailed examination of corruption and\nabuse, including the torturing of suspects, by Mississippi sheriffs and their\nofficers over two decades.\"  \nStaff of _[The Villages Daily\nSun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Villages_Daily_Sun \"The Villages Daily\nSun\")_ , \"for its comprehensive investigation and moment-by-moment account of\nFlorida officials\u2019 inaction before, during and after [Hurricane\nIan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hurricane_Ian \"Hurricane Ian\"), the\ndeadliest storm to strike the state since 1935.\"  \n[National\nReporting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pulitzer_Prize_for_National_Reporting\n\"Pulitzer Prize for National Reporting\")  \n---  \n**Staff of _[Reuters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reuters \"Reuters\")_** ,\n\"for an eye-opening series of accountability stories focused on [Elon\nMusk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elon_Musk \"Elon Musk\")\u2019s automobile and\naerospace businesses, stories that displayed remarkable breadth and depth and\nprovoked official probes of his companies\u2019 practices in Europe and the United\nStates.\" **Staff of _[The Washington\nPost](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Washington_Post \"The Washington\nPost\")_** , \"for its sobering examination of the AR-15 semi-automatic _[Associated\nPress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Associated_Press \"Associated Press\")_ ,\n\"for a deeply reported series on the corrosive effect of the pandemic on\npublic education, highlighting the staggering number of students missing from\nclassrooms across America.\"  \nDave Philipps of _The New York Times_ , \"for groundbreaking reporting that\nuncovered a pattern of traumatic brain injuries among U.S. troops from blast\nexposures caused by the weapons they were firing.\"  \n[International\nReporting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pulitzer_Prize_for_International_Reporting\n\"Pulitzer Prize for International Reporting\")  \n---  \n**Staff of _The New York Times_** , \"for its wide-ranging and revelatory\ncoverage of [Hamas\u2019 lethal attack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Hamas-\nled_attack_on_Israel \"2023 Hamas-led attack on Israel\") in southern Israel exposed the methodical undermining of the world\u2019s largest\ndemocracy by [Narendra Modi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Narendra_Modi\n\"Narendra Modi\") and his Hindu nationalist allies, who have deployed social\nmedia to foment hate and pressure American tech giants to bend to government\npower.\"
