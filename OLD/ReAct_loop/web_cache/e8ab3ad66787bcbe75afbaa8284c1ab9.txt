 \n  \n**Usher Raymond IV** (born October 14, 1978) is an American singer,\nsongwriter, dancer, and actor.\n\nUsher released his [self-titled debut\nalbum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Usher_\\(album\\) \"Usher \\(album\\)\") in\n1994, at 15. He rose to fame with the release of his second album, _[My\nWay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/My_Way_\\(Usher_album\\) \"My Way \\(Usher\nalbum\\)\")_ (1997), which spawned his first US [_Billboard_ Hot\n100](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_Hot_100 \"Billboard Hot 100\")\nnumber-one single \"[Nice & Slow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nice_&_Slow\n\"Nice & Slow\")\". His third album, _[8701](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/8701\n\"8701\")_ (2001), yielded the US number-one singles, \"[U Remind\nMe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U_Remind_Me \"U Remind Me\")\" and \"[U Got It\nBad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U_Got_It_Bad \"U Got It Bad\")\". Usher's\nfourth album,\n_[Confessions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confessions_\\(Usher_album\\)\n\"Confessions \\(Usher album\\)\")_ (2004), remains the [best-selling album by a\nBlack artist of the 21st century](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_best-\nselling_albums_of_the_21st_century \"List of best-selling albums of the 21st\ncentury\"). It was supported by four consecutive US number-one singles:\n\"[Yeah!](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yeah!_\\(Usher_song\\) \"Yeah! \\(Usher\nsong\\)\")\", \"[Burn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burn_\\(Usher_song\\) \"Burn\n\\(Usher song\\)\")\", \"[Confessions Part\nII](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confessions_Part_II \"Confessions Part II\")\",\nand \"[My\nBoo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/My_Boo_\\(Usher_and_Alicia_Keys_song\\) \"My\nBoo \\(Usher and Alicia Keys song\\)\")\". In 2007, Usher released the album\n_[Here I Stand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Here_I_Stand_\\(Usher_album\\)\n\"Here I Stand \\(Usher album\\)\")_ (2008), which spawned the US number-one\nsingle \"[Love in This Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Love_in_This_Club\n\"Love in This Club\")\". _Billboard_ named him the second-[most successful\nartist of the 2000s](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_Decade-End#2000s\n\"Billboard Decade-End\") and the number-one Hot 100 artist of the decade.\n\nUsher's sixth album, _[Raymond v.\nRaymond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raymond_v._Raymond \"Raymond v.\nRaymond\")_ (2010), contained his ninth number-one single,\n\"[OMG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OMG_\\(Usher_song\\) \"OMG \\(Usher\nsong\\)\")\", making him one of the few artists to top the _Billboard_ Hot 100 in\nthree consecutive decades. His debut [extended\nplay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extended_play \"Extended play\"),\n_[Versus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Versus_\\(Usher_EP\\) \"Versus \\(Usher\nEP\\)\")_ (2010), produced the top-five single \"[DJ Got Us Fallin' in\nLove](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DJ_Got_Us_Fallin'_in_Love \"DJ Got Us\nFallin' in Love\")\". Usher's seventh album, _[Looking 4\nMyself](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Looking_4_Myself \"Looking 4 Myself\")_\n(2012) saw further [electronic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_music\n\"Electronic music\") production and spawned the top-ten single\n\"[Scream](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scream_\\(Usher_song\\) \"Scream \\(Usher\nsong\\)\")\".
