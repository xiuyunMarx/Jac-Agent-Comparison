Roman architecture flourished in the [Roman\nRepublic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Republic \"Roman Republic\") and\nto an even greater extent under the\n[Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Empire \"Roman Empire\"), when the\ngreat majority of surviving buildings were constructed.
