 \n  \n**Zhejiang University** (**ZJU**) is a [public\nuniversity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_university \"Public\nuniversity\") in [Hangzhou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hangzhou \"Hangzhou\"),\nZhejiang, China. It is affiliated with the [Ministry of\nEducation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Education_\\(China\\)\n\"Ministry of Education \\(China\\)\"). The university is part of [Project\n211](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Project_211 \"Project 211\"), [Project\n985](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Project_985 \"Project 985\"), and [Double\nFirst-Class Construction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Double_First-\nClass_Construction \"Double First-Class Construction\").\n\nThe university was established as **National Third Chung Shan\nUniversity**[a][5] in 1927, in memory of Sun Yat-sen, and soon renamed as\n**National Chekiang University**[b] (**NCKU**) in 1928.[8] During the\npresidency of [Chu Kochen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chu_Coching \"Chu\nCoching\") from 1936 to 1949, the university retreated to\n[Guizhou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guizhou \"Guizhou\") in Western China\nduring the [Second Sino-Japanese\nWar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Sino-Japanese_War \"Second Sino-\nJapanese War\"),[9] before it moved back to Hangzhou in 1946. After the\n[Communist\nRevolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Communist_Revolution\n\"Chinese Communist Revolution\"), the university was [re-organized as an\nengineering-specialized\nuniversity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1952_reorganisation_of_Chinese_higher_education\n\"1952 reorganisation of Chinese higher education\") in 1952. In 1998, [Zhejiang\nMedical University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhejiang_Medical_University\n\"Zhejiang Medical University\"), [Hangzhou\nUniversity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hangzhou_University \"Hangzhou\nUniversity\") and [Zhejiang Agricultural\nUniversity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhejiang_Agricultural_University\n\"Zhejiang Agricultural University\"), which were derived from former\ndepartments of ZJU, merged and formed the present-day ZJU as a comprehensive\nuniversity.[10][11][7]\n\nThe university maintains seven faculties with 37 colleges and schools,\noffering about 140 undergraduate and 300 graduate programs.[12] The university\nalso has seven affiliated hospitals, one museum, and two international joint\ninstitutes.[13] Fifty-two members of ZJU faculty are also members of the\n[Chinese Academy of\nSciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Academy_of_Sciences \"Chinese\nAcademy of Sciences\") or the [Chinese Academy of\nEngineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Academy_of_Engineering\n\"Chinese Academy of Engineering\").[14] The university is a member of the [C9\nLeague](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C9_League \"C9 League\").\n\nZhejiang University was ranked first worldwide among academic institutions for\nscientific research output by the [Nature\nIndex](https://en.wikipedia.o"}
