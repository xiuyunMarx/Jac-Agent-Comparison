Wenfeng**\n([Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simplified_Chinese_characters\n\"Simplified Chinese characters\"): \u6881\u6587\u950b;\n[pinyin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pinyin \"Pinyin\"): _Li\u00e1ng W\u00e9nf\u0113ng_ ;\nborn 1985) is a Chinese\n[entrepreneur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entrepreneurship\n\"Entrepreneurship\") and\n[businessman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Businessperson \"Businessperson\")\nwho is the co-founder of the quantitative hedge fund [High-\nFlyer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High-Flyer \"High-Flyer\"), as well as the\nfounder and CEO of its [artificial\nintelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_intelligence\n\"Artificial intelligence\") company\n[DeepSeek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DeepSeek \"DeepSeek\").[1]\n\nLiang attended [Zhejiang\nUniversity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhejiang_University \"Zhejiang\nUniversity\"), and began his career by applying machine learning methods to\nquantitative finance. Through High-Flyer, he built large-scale computing\ninfrastructure that was later used to support artificial intelligence\nresearch, leading to the creation of DeepSeek in 2023. DeepSeek gained\ninternational attention following the release of DeepSeek-R1, which analysts\ndescribed as demonstrating high-level performance with comparatively limited\ncompute resources.\n\nIn 2025, Liang was named to [_Time_\nmagazine's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_\\(magazine\\) \"Time\n\\(magazine\\)\") list of 100 Most Influential People in AI and\n_[Fortune's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fortune_\\(magazine\\) \"Fortune\n\\(magazine\\)\")_ list of the Most Powerful People in Business.\n\nIn July 2026, [Bloomberg News](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bloomberg_News\n\"Bloomberg News\") reported that Liang had become the world's wealthiest\nfounder of an AI-model company, with a net worth of $36 billion, according to\nthe [Bloomberg Billionaires\nIndex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bloomberg_Billionaires_Index \"Bloomberg\nBillionaires Index\").[2]\n\n## Early life\n\n[[edit](/w/index.php?title=Liang_Wenfeng&action=edit&section=1 \"Edit section:\nEarly life\")]\n\nLiang was born in 1985 in the village of Mililing (\u7c73\u5386\u5cad\u6751), Qinba town (\u8983\u5df4\u9547),\n[Wuchuan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wuchuan,_Guangdong \"Wuchuan,\nGuangdong\"), [Guangdong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guangdong\n\"Guangdong\").[3] His parents were both primary school teachers.[4][5][6][7][3]\nLiang was routinely praised by both locals and teachers alike.[3] Even since\nmiddle school, Liang was recalled for being well-known for reading comic\nbooks, while also being very proficient in mathematics.[3]\n\n## Education\n\n[[edit](/w/index.php?title=Liang_Wenfeng&action=edit&section=2 \"Edit section:\nEducation\")]\n\nAfter elementary school, Liang attended Wuchuan No. 1 Middle School. There, he\nquickly excelled in class and ranked highly amongst his peers. He taught\nhimself high school and university-level mathematics courses.[8]\n\nLiang then attended Wuchaun No. 1 High School. In these years, he developed\nhobbies of mathematical modeling and conducting research projects. Compared to\nhis peers, he was always ranked highly. For every mathematics exam, he always\nranked within the top three. He was also the top scorer in the\n[Zhanjiang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhanjiang \"Zhanjiang\") region of\nGuangdong for the [college entrance exam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaokao\n\"Gaokao\").[9] With a score of 806, he met the admission requirements for\n[Tsinghua University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsinghua_University\n\"Tsinghua University\") but ultimately chose to attend [Zhejiang\nUniversity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhejiang_University \"Zhejiang\nUniversity\") due to the major offered.[10] Thus, in 2002, Liang left high\nschool early to further pursue his education at the university level at the\nyoung age of 17.[3]\n\nAttending university at the age of 17, Liang earned a [Bachelor of\nEngineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Engineering \"Bachelor\nof Engineering\") in Electronic Information Engineering in 2007 and his [Master\nof Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Engineering \"Master of\nEngineering\") in Information & Communication Engineering in 2010.[11] His\n[master's dissertation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master's_dissertation\n\"Master's dissertation\") was titled \"Study on Object Tracking Algorithm Based\non Low-Cost [PTZ camera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pan\u2013tilt\u2013zoom_camera\n\"Pan\u2013tilt\u2013zoom camera\")\" (\u57fa\u4e8e\u4f4e\u6210\u672cPTZ\u6444\u50cf\u673a\u7684\u76ee\u6807\u8ddf\u8e2a\u7b97\u6cd5\u7814\u7a76).[5][12]\n\nIn his college years, [DJI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DJI \"DJI\") founder\n[Wang Tao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frank_Wang \"Frank Wang\") asked Liang\nto join as a co-founder. Liang declined the invitation to pursue artificial\nintelligence methodologies in financial markets.[12] While he states that\nthose around him had entrepreneurial mindsets, he himself valued\nacademics.[13]\n\n## Career\n\n[[edit](/w/index.php?title=Liang_Wenfeng&action=edit&section=3 \"Edit section:\nCareer\")]\n\n### Early career (2008\u20132016)\n\n[[edit](/w/index.php?title=Liang_Wenfeng&action=edit&section=4 \"Edit section:\nEarly career \\(2008\u20132016\\)\")]\n\nDuring the [2008 financial\ncrisis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008_financial_crisis \"2008 financial\ncrisis\"), Liang formed a team with his classmates to accumulate data related\nto financial markets. He also led the team to explore [quantitative\ntrading](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantitative_trading \"Quantitative\ntrading\") using [machine\nlearning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Machine_learning \"Machine learning\")\nand other technologies.[14][15][16]\n\nAfter his graduation, Liang moved to a cheap flat in\n[Chengdu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chengdu \"Chengdu\"),\n[Sichuan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sichuan \"Sichuan\"), where he\nexperimented with ways to apply AI to various fields. These ventures failed,\nuntil he tried applying AI to finance.[17]\n\nIn 2013, Liang attempted to integrate artificial intelligence with\nquantitative trading and founded Hangzhou Yakebi Investment Management Co Ltd\nwith Xu Jin, an alumnus of Zhejiang University. In 2015, they co-founded\nHangzhou Huanfang Technology Co Ltd, which is today's Zhejiang Jiuzhang Asset\nManagement Co Ltd.[18]\n\n### High-Flyer (2016\u20132023)\n\n[[edit](/w/index.php?title=Liang_Wenfeng&action=edit&section=5 \"Edit section:\nHigh-Flyer \\(2016\u20132023\\)\")]\n\nIn February 2016, Liang and two other engineering classmates co-founded\n[Ningbo High-Flyer Quantitative Investment Management Partnership (Limited\nPartnership)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High-Flyer \"High-\nFlyer\").[17][18][19] The team relied on mathematics and AI to make\ninvestments.[14][20][21] Much of the early startup culture was described by\nformer employees to be \"geeky\" and \"quirky,\" often seen as contrary to the\nexisting culture in large Chinese tech companies.[22]\n\nIn 2019, Liang founded High-Flyer AI which was dedicated to research on AI\nalgorithms and its basic applications.[14][17] By this time, High-Flyer had\nover 10 billion yuan in [assets under\nmanagement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assets_under_management \"Assets\nunder management\").[14]\n\nOn 30 August 2019, Liang Wenfeng delivered a keynote speech entitled \"The\nFuture of Quantitative Investment in China from a Programmer's Perspective\" at\nthe Private Equity Golden Bull Award ceremony held by _[China Securities\nJournal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China_Securities_Journal \"China\nSecurities Journal\")_ , and sparked heated discussions. Liang stated that the\ncriterion for determining what is quantitative or non-quantitative is whether\nthe investment decision is made by quantitative methods or by people.\nQuantitative funds do not have [portfolio\nmanagers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portfolio_manager \"Portfolio manager\")\nmaking the decisions and instead are just servers. He also stated High-Flyer's\nmission is to improve the effectiveness of China's [secondary\nmarket](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secondary_market \"Secondary\nmarket\").[14]\n\nIn February 2021, [Gregory\nZuckerman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gregory_Zuckerman \"Gregory\nZuckerman\")'s book _The Man Who Solved the Market: How[Jim\nSimons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jim_Simons \"Jim Simons\") Launched the\nQuant Revolution_ was published. Liang wrote the preface for the Chinese\nedition of the book where he stated that whenever he encountered difficulties\nat work, he would think of Simons' words \"There must be a way to model\nprices\".[14] In January 2025, Zuckerman wrote in _[The Wall Street\nJournal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Wall_Street_Journal \"The Wall\nStreet Journal\")_ where he acknowledged this fact and stated he has been\ntrying to get in touch with Liang but much like Simons, Liang is very\nsecretive and difficult to contact.[23]\n\nDuring 2021, Liang started buying thousands of\n[Nvidia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nvidia \"Nvidia\")\n[GPUs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GPUs \"GPUs\") for his\n[AI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_intelligence \"Artificial\nintelligence\") side project while running High-Flyer. Liang wanted to build\nsomething and it will be a game changer which his business partners thought\nwas only possible from giants such as\n[ByteDance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ByteDance \"ByteDance\") and [Alibaba\nGroup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alibaba_Group \"Alibaba Group\").[24]\n\n### DeepSeek (since 2023)\n\n[[edit](/w/index.php?title=Liang_Wenfeng&action=edit&section=6 \"Edit section:\nDeepSeek \\(since 2023\\)\")]\n\n#### DeepSeek begins\n\n[[edit](/w/index.php?title=Liang_Wenfeng&action=edit&section=7 \"Edit section:\nDeepSeek begins\")]\n\nIn May 2023, Liang announced High-Flyer would pursue the development of\n[artificial general\nintelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_general_intelligence\n\"Artificial general intelligence\") and launched\n[DeepSeek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DeepSeek \"DeepSeek\").[20][25][17][26]\nDuring that month in an interview with\n[36Kr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/36Kr \"36Kr\"), Liang stated that High-\nFlyer had acquired 10,000 [Nvidia\nA100](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nvidia_A100 \"Nvidia A100\") GPUs before the\nUS government imposed AI chip restrictions on China. That laid the foundation\nfor DeepSeek to operate as an\n[LLM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Large_language_model \"Large language\nmodel\") developer.\n\nLiang also stated DeepSeek gets funding from High-Flyer.[17][27] This was\nbecause when DeepSeek was founded, [venture\ncapital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venture_capital \"Venture capital\")\nfirms were reluctant in providing funding as it was unlikely that it would be\nable to generate an [exit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exit_\\(investing\\)\n\"Exit \\(investing\\)\") in a short period of time.
