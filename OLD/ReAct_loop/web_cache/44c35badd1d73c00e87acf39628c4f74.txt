\"Single-\nplayer\"), [multiplayer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multiplayer\n\"Multiplayer\")  \n  \n_**Call of Duty: Black Ops 6**_ is a 2024 [first-person\nshooter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First-person_shooter \"First-person\nshooter\") video game co-developed by\n[Treyarch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treyarch \"Treyarch\") and [Raven\nSoftware](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raven_Software \"Raven Software\") and\npublished by [Activision](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Activision\n\"Activision\"). It is the twenty-first main installment of the _[Call of\nDuty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Call_of_Duty \"Call of Duty\")_ series and\nis the seventh entry in the [_Black\nOps_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Call_of_Duty#Black_Ops_series \"Call of\nDuty\") sub-series, following _[Call of Duty: Black Ops Cold\nWar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Call_of_Duty:_Black_Ops_Cold_War \"Call of\nDuty: Black Ops Cold War\")_ (2020). Set during [Operation Desert\nStorm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gulf_War \"Gulf War\"), _Black Ops 6_ 's\n[single-player](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Single-player \"Single-player\")\nstory follows rogue\n[CIA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Intelligence_Agency \"Central\nIntelligence Agency\") operatives Troy Marshall and Frank Woods as they\nassemble a team of agents to hunt down Pantheon, a\n[paramilitary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paramilitary \"Paramilitary\")\ngroup with covert ties to the agency. As with previous _Call of Duty_ titles,\nthe game also includes a\n[multiplayer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multiplayer \"Multiplayer\")\ncomponent and a [new\niteration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Call_of_Duty:_Black_Ops_6_Zombies\n\"Call of Duty: Black Ops 6 Zombies\") of the\n[cooperative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cooperative_video_game\n\"Cooperative video game\") round-based [Zombies\nmode](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Call_of_Duty_Zombies \"Call of Duty\nZombies\").\n\n_Black Ops 6_ had a four-year development cycle\u2014the longest in _Call of Duty_\nhistory; a portion of the game's production was spent on rebuilding core\naspects of _Black Ops_ within the latest version of the _Call of Duty_ [game\nengine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Game_engine \"Game engine\"). An\nomnidirectional movement system was also introduced, allowing players to\nsprint, dive, and slide in any direction. Marketing for _Black Ops 6_ began in\nMay 2024, through the release of [live-\naction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Live_action \"Live action\") teaser\ntrailers and the publishing of advertisements on the front pages of multiple\nnewspaper outlets; a full reveal debuted during the 2024 [Xbox Games\nShowcase](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xbox_Developer_Direct \"Xbox Developer\nDirect\") event on June 9. The game was released on October 25, 2024, for\n[PlayStation 4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PlayStation_4 \"PlayStation 4\"),\n[PlayStation 5](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PlayStation_5 \"PlayStation 5\"),\n[Windows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Windows \"Windows\"), [Xbox\nOne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xbox_One \"Xbox One\"), and [Xbox Series\nX/S](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xbox_Series_X_and_Series_S \"Xbox Series X\nand Series S\").\n\nUpon release, _Black Ops 6_ received generally favorable reviews from critics,\nachieved the largest launch weekend in the franchise's history, and was\nnominated for several accolades. All three modes were met with praise, with\ncritics positively highlighting the new omnidirectional movement system. A\nsequel, titled _[Call of Duty: Black Ops\n7](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Call_of_Duty:_Black_Ops_7 \"Call of Duty:\nBlack Ops 7\")_ , was released in November 2025.\n\n## Gameplay\n\n[[edit](/w/index.php?title=Call_of_Duty:_Black_Ops_6&action=edit&section=1\n\"Edit section: Gameplay\")]\n\n[![Visual demonstration of Call of Duty: Black Ops 6's omnidirectional\nmovement\nsystem.](//thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/thumb/9/96/Black_Ops_6_Omnimovement.gif/250px-\nBlack_Ops_6_Omnimovement.gif?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Black_Ops_6_Omnimovement.gif)New\nto _Call of Duty: Black Ops 6_ is omnidirectional movement, allowing players\nto sprint, dive, and slide in any direction. The above clip demonstrates the\nomnidirectional sprinting ability.\n\n_Call of Duty: Black Ops 6_ is a [first-person\nshooter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First-person_shooter \"First-person\nshooter\").[3] Depending on the [game\nmode](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Game_mode \"Game mode\") selected, players\nuse a selection of military equipment, such as weapons, \"field upgrades\", and\n\"scorestreaks\", to combat AI opponents, other human players, or [undead\ncreatures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zombie \"Zombie\").[3][4][5] Players\ncan perform a wide variety of movement actions when traversing a\n[level/map](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Level_\\(video_games\\) \"Level \\(video\ngames\\)\"), including walking, crouching, sprinting, jumping, sliding, and\ndiving;[6] while sprinting, players can initiate a \"tactical sprint\", which\nfurther increases the player's movement speed for a limited duration.[7] In a\nchange from previous _[Call of\nDuty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Call_of_Duty \"Call of Duty\")_ titles,\nplayers have omnidirectional control over the sprint, dive, and slide\nmechanics, allowing them to perform these actions in any direction; players\ncan also rotate themselves in a 360-degree view while they are laying in the\n[prone position](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prone_position \"Prone\nposition\").[1] In addition, a series of \"Intelligent Movement\" settings are\nalso available to players, which automate certain movement actions, including\nsprinting and mantling.[8]\n\n_Black Ops 6_ features three main game modes: a [single-\nplayer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Single-player \"Single-player\") campaign,\na [multiplayer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multiplayer \"Multiplayer\")\ncomponent and a [new\niteration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Call_of_Duty:_Black_Ops_6_Zombies\n\"Call of Duty: Black Ops 6 Zombies\") of the\n[cooperative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cooperative_video_game\n\"Cooperative video game\") round-based [Zombies\nmode](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Call_of_Duty_Zombies \"Call of Duty\nZombies\").[2] In the campaign, players assume control of a [silent\nprotagonist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silent_protagonist \"Silent\nprotagonist\"), \"Case\", as he and a team of rogue\n[CIA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Intelligence_Agency \"Central\nIntelligence Agency\") operatives work to hunt down Pantheon, a\n[paramilitary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paramilitary \"Paramilitary\")\ngroup with covert ties to the agency.[9] The campaign features 11\nmissions,[10] and is estimated to take at least 8 hours to complete.[11]\nPlayers typically have multiple ways to approach a mission's objectives; some\nmissions include [stealth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stealth_game \"Stealth\ngame\") elements.[9] In-between missions, players are able to access a [safe\nhouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Safe_house \"Safe house\"), where they can\nselect new missions via an [evidence\nboard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evidence_board \"Evidence board\"),\ninteract with [non-player characters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-\nplayer_character \"Non-player character\") (NPCs),[1] and spend cash, which is\nfound throughout missions, on various in-game perks; these are split across\nthree categories, which are related to the player's physical abilities,\nweapons, and gear.[9] Certain campaign missions reward players with cosmetic\nitems for use in the multiplayer and Zombies modes upon their completion,\nincluding weapon \"blueprints\", player skins,\n[stickers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sticker \"Sticker\"), and weapon\ncharms.[12]\n\nThe multiplayer component puts two opposing teams\u2014\"Rogue Black Ops\" and\n\"Crimson One\"\u2014against each other across a wide selection of game modes,[13]\nsuch as [Team\nDeathmatch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deathmatch_\\(video_games\\)#Variations\n\"Deathmatch \\(video games\\)\"), Domination, Hardpoint, and Kill Order, most of\nwhich allow for six-versus-six combat; an exception to this is the round-based\n\"Gunfight\" mode, which puts two teams of two on small-sized maps with\nrandomized loadouts.[14] Like previous _Call of Duty_ games, player\nperformance is tracked with [experience\npoints](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Experience_point \"Experience point\")\n(XP), which can be earned by killing enemy players, completing objectives, or\nfinishing challenges; XP can also be earned in the Zombies mode.[15] As\nplayers rank up, they gain access to various loadout items, including new\nweapons, pieces of equipment, and perks,[16] the latter of which are split\ninto three \"combat specialties\"; selecting three perks of the same specialty\ngrants access to in-game buffs.[17] Once players reach level 55, they can\nchoose to enter the \"Prestige\" ranking system, which resets their progress\nback to level 1 but grants a set of cosmetic rewards and a token that can be\nused to permanently unlock any loadout item. This process can be done up to\nten times; once players complete the tenth Prestige, they are granted the\nability to rank up to level 1000, and if they do so, they receive"}
