Awards** ceremony, presented by the [Academy of Motion\nPicture Arts and\nSciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_Motion_Picture_Arts_and_Sciences\n\"Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences\") (AMPAS), took place on March\n10, 2024, at the [Dolby Theatre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dolby_Theatre\n\"Dolby Theatre\") in\n[Hollywood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hollywood,_Los_Angeles \"Hollywood,\nLos Angeles\"), Los Angeles. During the gala, the AMPAS presented Academy\nAwards (commonly referred to as Oscars) in 23 categories honoring [films\nreleased in 2023](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_in_film \"2023 in film\").\nThe ceremony, televised in the United States by\n[ABC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Broadcasting_Company \"American\nBroadcasting Company\"), was produced by Raj Kapoor and Katy Mullan, and was\ndirected by [Hamish\nHamilton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamish_Hamilton_\\(director\\) \"Hamish\nHamilton \\(director\\)\").[2][5] Comedian [Jimmy\nKimmel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jimmy_Kimmel \"Jimmy Kimmel\") hosted the\nshow for the fourth time. He first presided over the [89th\nceremony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/89th_Academy_Awards \"89th Academy\nAwards\") held in 2017, and had most recently hosted the [previous year's\nceremony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/95th_Academy_Awards \"95th Academy\nAwards\").[6]\n\nIn related events, the Academy held its 14th [Governors\nAwards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governors_Awards \"Governors Awards\")\nceremony at the Ray Dolby Ballroom of the [Ovation\nHollywood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ovation_Hollywood \"Ovation\nHollywood\") complex in Hollywood, on January 9, 2024.[7] The [Academy\nScientific and Technical\nAwards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Scientific_and_Technical_Award\n\"Academy Scientific and Technical Award\") were presented by host [Natasha\nLyonne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natasha_Lyonne \"Natasha Lyonne\") on\nFebruary 23, 2024, in a ceremony at the [Academy Museum of Motion\nPictures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Museum_of_Motion_Pictures\n\"Academy Museum of Motion Pictures\") in Los Angeles.[8]\n\n_[Oppenheimer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oppenheimer_\\(film\\) \"Oppenheimer\n\\(film\\)\")_ won seven awards, including [Best\nPicture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Picture \"Academy\nAward for Best Picture\").[9] Other winners included
