facility\n\n**Lineage, Inc.** (formerly **Lineage Logistics**) is the world's largest\ntemperature-controlled warehouse [real estate investment trust\n(REIT)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_estate_investment_trust \"Real\nestate investment trust\"), owned by Bay Grove, LLC.[3][4][5] Entering\ninternational markets in 2017, Lineage grew into the world's largest\nrefrigerated warehousing company with a capacity exceeding 3 billion cubic\nfeet and acquiring more than 100 companies through the end of 2023.[6][7]\nLineage operates over 450 facilities across 18 countries, with approximately\n26,000 team members globally in North America, Europe, and Asia Pacific.[8][9]\n\n## History\n\n[[edit](/w/index.php?title=Lineage_\\(company\\)&action=edit&section=1 \"Edit\nsection: History\")]\n\nThe company was founded in April 2012 by former Morgan Stanley investment\nbanker Adam Forste, who formed Bay Grove Capital, LLC, through the\nconsolidation of acquired warehousing and logistics companies beginning, in\nDecember 2008, with the purchase of Seafreeze from [Toyo Susian\nKaisha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toyo_Suisan \"Toyo Suisan\").[10] The\ncompany then acquired CityIce, in 2009, and Flint River Services, in 2010,\nfollowed, in 2011, by Terminal Freezers of [Santa Maria,\nCalifornia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Maria,_California \"Santa\nMaria, California\"), and Richmond Cold Storage, purchased from Atlanta Equity.\n\nLineage was formed with the fifth-largest network of temperature-controlled\nwarehouse facilities in North America in 2012, according to [International\nAssociation of Refrigerated\nWarehouses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Association_of_Refrigerated_Warehouses\n\"International Association of Refrigerated Warehouses\") (IARW) rankings, with\n40 facilities in eight states, including near international ports at\n[Savannah, Georgia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Savannah,_Georgia \"Savannah,\nGeorgia\"), [Seattle,\nWashington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seattle,_Washington \"Seattle,\nWashington\") and [Norfolk,\nVirginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norfolk,_Virginia \"Norfolk,\nVirginia\"); as well as providing warehouse management, consulting, and food\nprocessing support.[10]\n\nIn a March 2021 interview with Bloomberg, Lineage CEO Greg Lehmkuhl said the\ncompany is actively preparing its [initial public\noffering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Initial_public_offering \"Initial\npublic offering\") (IPO).[5] The company completed its IPO in July 2024 with a\nlisting on the [Nasdaq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nasdaq \"Nasdaq\"),\nraising around $4.5 billion, valuing the company at over $18 billion.[11]\n\nOn April 21, 2024, a fire broke out at the Lineage cold storage facility in\n[Benton County,\nWashington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benton_County,_Washington \"Benton\nCounty, Washington\"). The fire burned for 60 days.[12]\n\nOn June 17, 2026 a [large\nfire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Boyle_Heights_warehouse_fire \"2026\nBoyle Heights warehouse fire\") broke out at the Lineage cold storage facility\nin [Boyle Heights, Los\nAngeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boyle_Heights,_Los_Angeles \"Boyle\nHeights, Los Angeles\"), accelerated by the large facility's [solar\npanels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solar_panel \"Solar panel\") encompassing\nits entire roof, refrigerants, and decaying food within the building. The fire\ncontinued into a fourth day, when a [state of\nemergency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_of_emergency \"State of\nemergency\") was declared due to the fire continuing to flare up and affecting\nregional air quality.[13] Shelter-in-place orders were issued in surrounding\nareas due to the commercial building fire.[14]\n\n## Operations and technology\n\n[[edit](/w/index.php?title=Lineage_\\(company\\)&action=edit&section=2 \"Edit\nsection: Operations and technology\")]\n\nBy 2015, Lineage's facility network was ranked the second-largest in the world\nby the International Association of Refrigerated Warehouses. With 111\nfacilities in 21 states, Lineage also ranked as the second largest\ntemperature-controlled warehousing and logistics company in the United\nStates.[15]\n\nIn September 2020, Lineage raised $1.6 billion in equity for technology and\nexpansion.[16]\n\nIn March 2021, Lineage raised $1.9 billion in equity for global [greenfield\ndevelopments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greenfield_project \"Greenfield\nproject\"), facility expansions, [M&A](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M&A \"M&A\")\nand [research and\ndevelopment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research_and_development \"Research\nand development\").[17]\n\nIn July 2021, Lineage partnered with 8VC logistics technology venture capital\nfirm, to further invest in the transportation and logistics technology\nsector.[18]\n\n[![](//thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/d/de/LINX_6684.jpg/250px-\nLINX_6684.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:LINX_6684.jpg)A\nLineage [boxcar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boxcar \"Boxcar\")\n\nIn November 2023, Lineage expanded and established\n[Harnes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harnes \"Harnes\") ([Pas-de-\nCalais](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pas-de-Calais \"Pas-de-Calais\")) as its\nEuropean logistics hub by opening a new 17,000 m2 (180,000 sq ft) state-of-\nthe-art modern cold storage facility.[19]\n\nAs of March 2024, Lineage has been granted over 75 patents registered across\nmultiple jurisdictions, related to technological developments within the\ntemperature-controlled logistics sector.[20]\n\n## Acquisitions and expansion\n\n[[edit](/w/index.php?title=Lineage_\\(company\\)&action=edit&section=3 \"Edit\nsection: Acquisitions and expansion\")]\n\nIn 2012, the company acquired Stanford Refrigerated Warehouses and Castle &\nCooke Cold Storage; Bill Hendricksen of Castle & Cooke then joined Lineage as\n[CEO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CEO \"CEO\").[21] Hendricksen was succeeded\nby W. Gregory Lehmkuhl, in July 2015.[15] Lineage subsequently moved its\nheadquarters from [Irvine,\nCalifornia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irvine,_California \"Irvine,\nCalifornia\") to Novi, Michigan.[22][23]\n\nIn April 2013, the company received a $220 million loan,[24] and acquired\nSeattle Cold Storage and a [University Park,\nIllinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_Park,_Illinois \"University\nPark, Illinois\") facility.[25] By 2014, most Lineage customers were food\nproducers requiring [cold chain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cold_chain\n\"Cold chain\") warehousing, transportation, and logistics; to meet demand, the\ncompany concentrated on automation and acquisition.[26] In March 2014, the\ncompany agreed to purchase Millard Refrigerated Services for about $1 billion,\nits largest acquisition to date,[26] making Lineage \"the second-largest\ntemperature-controlled warehousing and logistics company in the world.\"[27] In\nthe same year, Lineage acquired Loop Cold Storage, Oneida Cold Storage &\nWarehouse, Murphy Overseas, and two facilities in Watsonville, California from\nDreisbach Enterprises and in September 2014, opened a new temperature-\ncontrolled warehouse in Santa Maria, California.[4]\n\nIn January 2015, Lineage acquired the Pacific Northwest cold storage\nfacilities of Columbia Colstor, headquartered in Moses Lake, Washington.[28]\n\nIn May 2016, the company opened a $150 million temperature-controlled\nwarehouse in [North Charleston, South\nCarolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Charleston,_South_Carolina\n\"North Charleston, South Carolina\"), near the [Port of\nCharleston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port_of_Charleston \"Port of\nCharleston\"),[29] then acquired Consolidated Distribution Corporation of\n[Lombard, Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lombard,_Illinois \"Lombard,\nIllinois\") that August,[15] making Lineage the largest customized\nredistribution network in the United States.[30] Two months later, it was\nreported that Lineage was considering locating a large facility at [New\nCentury AirCenter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Century_AirCenter \"New\nCentury AirCenter\") in [Gardner,\nKansas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gardner,_Kansas \"Gardner, Kansas\"),[31]\nbut the proposed location resulted in lawsuits from local residents,[31] and\nLineage scrapped the project in November 2017 \"due to a change in customer\nneeds\",[31][32] though the project had received unanimous approval from county\ncommissioners in July.[31][32]\n\nLineage entered European markets in 2017, acquiring Partner Logistics, a large\ncold-chain storage supplier.[25] The company purchased facilities from\nAmerican Cold Storage, in the\n[Midwest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Midwestern_United_States \"Midwestern\nUnited States\"), and eight [Los Angeles,\nCalifornia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles,_California \"Los\nAngeles, California\") area warehouses from U.S. Growers Cold Storage,[3]\nraising its employee roster to 7,200, with Lineage operating 120 temperature-\ncontrolled storage facilities.[10]\n\nThe company then expanded into the temperature-controlled logistics markets in\nAustralia, New Zealand, and Sri Lanka, in November 2019, with the acquisition\nof Emergent Cold,[33] then into [Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada\n\"Canada\"), in July 2020, acquiring Ontario Refrigerated Services Inc.[34]\n\nDuring 2018, the company acquired 24 companies, including The Yearsley Group,\nthe UK's largest Cold Storage and Haulage supplier, maintaining its second-\nlargest status.[35] The company also sold minority stakes worth $700 million.\nCompany real estate holdings were estimated at $4 billion in value.[6]\n\nAlso in 2018, [Stonepeak\nPartners](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stonepeak_Partners \"Stonepeak\nPartners\"), D1 Capital Partners, and existing backers acquired minority stakes\nin the company valued at $700 million.[6]\n\nOn February 25, 2019 _[The Wall Street\nJournal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Wall_Street_Journal \"The Wall\nStreet Journal\")_ reported the company's [acquisition of Preferred Freezer\nServices LLC.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2800_Polar_Way \"2800 Polar Way\"),\nits largest competitor, in a deal reportedly worth over $1 billion, expanding\nLineage to the world's largest facility network, with 1.3 billion cubic feet\nof storage in over 200 facilities across the United States, Europe, and\nAsia.[8][36]\n\nOn May 22, 2020 Lineage acquired the distribution assets of one of the largest\nindependent Food Distribution companies in the country for over 100 years,\nMaines Paper & Food, Inc, based in [Conklin, New\nYork](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conklin,_New_York \"Conklin, New\nYork\").[36] That June, the company acquired Emergent Cold, Australia's largest\ncold-chain supplier, and New Orleans Cold Storage, with four port facilities\nin [New Orleans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Orleans \"New Orleans\"),\nLouisiana, [Houston, Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Houston,_Texas\n\"Houston, Texas\") and [Charleston, South\nCarolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charleston,_South_Carolina\n\"Charleston, South Carolina\").[33]\n\nIn July, Lineage acquired Henningsen Cold Storage, based in [Portland,\nOregon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portland,_Oregon \"Portland, Oregon\"),\nwith 14 cold storage facilities located mainly in Oregon and Washington,\nraising its employee roster to 16,000.[37] This acquisition raised Lineage's\nglobal storage capacity to 50,660,000 m3 (1.789\u00d7109 cu ft).[38]\n\nOn December 1, 2020, Lineage acquired Pago, a warehousing, distribution and\ntransport logistics provider in Poland.[39]\n\nIn May 2021, Lineage acquired Crystal Creek e-commerce fulfillment logistics\noperator and its five U.S. warehouses,[40] increasing the company's\nacquisitions to 72.[41] The same month, Lineage entered the Spanish\nmarketplace, acquiring cold storage firm Frigorificos de Navarra of\n[Gij\u00f3n](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gij\u00f3n \"Gij\u00f3n\"), Spain from Frioastur\nand, in June 2021, entered the European freight industry with its acquisition\nof [Rotterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotterdam \"Rotterdam\")-based UTI\nForwarding,[42] and also acquired Kloosterboer Group in Europe, and the cold\nstorage division of Claus Sorensen Group in Denmark.[43] In November 2021,\nLineage expanded into the Italian cold storage market with the acquisition of\nKantaro SRL.[44]\n\nIn January 2022, Lineage continued its expansion by acquiring
