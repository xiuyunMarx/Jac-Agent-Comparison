of\n[Fasano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fasano \"Fasano\") in Apulia, Italy.[1]\n\nPrime Minister [Giorgia Meloni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giorgia_Meloni\n\"Giorgia Meloni\") announced in November 2023 that Fasano would be the\nheadquarters of the G7.\n\n## Leaders at the summit\n\n[[edit](/w/index.php?title=50th_G7_summit&action=edit&section=1 \"Edit section:\nLeaders at the summit\")]\n\n[![](//thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/9/96/Giorgia_Meloni_Official_2024_%28cropped%29.jpg/250px-\nGiorgia_Meloni_Official_2024_%28cropped%29.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Giorgia_Meloni_Official_2024_\\(cropped\\).jpg)[Giorgia\nMeloni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giorgia_Meloni \"Giorgia Meloni\") chaired\nthe 50th G7 summit.\n\n### Background\n\n[[edit](/w/index.php?title=50th_G7_summit&action=edit&section=2 \"Edit section:\nBackground\")]\n\nAll G7 member states participated in the summit, including the representatives\nof the European Union. The president of the European Commission has been a\npermanently welcome participant at all meetings since 1981.\n\nItaly, coinciding with its [G7](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/G7 \"G7\")\npresidency in 2024, emphasized strategic [partnerships with\nAfrica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Italy\u2013Africa_Summit \"2024\nItaly\u2013Africa Summit\").[2]\n\nThis was the first time that a pope has attended a G7 summit.[3]\n\nThe 2024 summit was the final summit for European Council President [Charles\nMichel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Michel \"Charles Michel\"),\nBritish Prime Minister [Rishi Sunak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rishi_Sunak\n\"Rishi Sunak\"), Canadian Prime Minister [Justin\nTrudeau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Trudeau \"Justin Trudeau\"),\n[German Chancellor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chancellor_of_Germany\n\"Chancellor of Germany\") [Olaf\nScholz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olaf_Scholz \"Olaf Scholz\"), Japanese\nPrime Minister [Fumio Kishida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fumio_Kishida\n\"Fumio Kishida\"), and U.S. President [Joe\nBiden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_Biden \"Joe Biden\").[_[citation\nneeded](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed\n\"Wikipedia:Citation needed\")_]\n\n### Participants and representatives\n\n[[edit](/w/index.php?title=50th_G7_summit&action=edit&section=3 \"Edit section:\nParticipants and representatives\")]\n\n[Core G7 Members](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/G7 \"G7\")  \nThe host state and leader are shown in bold text.
