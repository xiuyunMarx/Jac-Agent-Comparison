Winners will be announced December 05, 2024.\n\n## Opening Round: Nov 12 - 24\n\nIn the first round there are 20 books in each of the 15 categories, and\nmembers can vote for one book in each category.\n\n## Final Round: Nov 26 - Dec 01\n\nThe field narrows to the top 10 books in each category, and members have one\nlast chance to vote!\n\n# 2024 Eligibility\n\nBooks published in the United States in English, including works in\ntranslation and other significant rereleases, between November 16, 2023, and\nNovember 12, 2024, are eligible for the 2024 Goodreads Choice Awards. Books\npublished between November 13, 2024, and November 11, 2025, will be eligible\nfor the 2025 awards.\n\nWe analyze statistics from the millions of books added, rated, and reviewed on\nGoodreads to nominate 20 books in each category. For the Audiobook category,\nnominations are based on the millions of titles added, rated, and reviewed\nboth on Goodreads and Audible.\n\nOpening round official nominees must have an average rating of 3.50 or higher\nat the time of launch. A book may be nominated in no more than one genre\ncategory, but can also be nominated in the Debut Novel and/or Audiobook\ncategories. Only one book in a series may be nominated per category. An author\nmay receive multiple nominations within a single category if they have more\nthan one eligible series or more than one eligible stand-alone book.
