She wonders what it was that made her\n> realize there was somewhere else to go._\n\n  \n  \nWhile _The Wedding People_ wasn't a perfect read for me, it certainly was a\ndarn special one.  \n  \nPhoebe has always wanted to go to the grand Cornwall Inn in Newport, and now\nshe is finally here. But it isn't at all the vacation she spent years dreaming\nof. For one, she isn't going with her husband because he is no longer her\nhusband. For another, she didn't bring any luggage because, well, you'll have\nto read the book for that. And just as she has resigned herself to her\ndecision, she unexpectedly meets the wedding people, all there to attend a\nweek-long celebration. Soon she is swept up in their exuberance and drama, and\njust maybe this is the vacation she was meant to take after all.  \n  \nThis is definitely one of those books you read for the journey, not for the\noutcome. It's pretty clear right from the beginning where this story is going\nto go, because how else would you have a story at all? To follow Phoebe as she\nmeets these strangers, see how they touch her life at exactly the moment she\nneeds it, while she in turn touches theirs, brings a lump to my throat and a\nflutter to my heart.  \n  \nThere is something so inherently loveable and relatable about Phoebe, even if\nwe have not all suffered the exact same terrible setbacks in life that she\nhas. Her candidness and vulnerability seems like a breath of fresh air,\ninviting the reader into her heart and her mind, and we have no choice but to\nfollow along.  \n  \nNo doubt Phoebe has been through a lot, more than anyone can be expected to\nendure. And yet, there is a quiet bravery and courage in how she approaches\nher life and in how the author chose to portray her. I can see how at the\nhands of a lesser wordsmith, this story could've easily turned maudlin or\npreachy, but no such fears here. For a story about such sad topics, I felt\nremarkably buoyant and hopeful while reading this.  \n  \nThere was so much heart and humor in here, I was often nodding and laughing\nalong, surprised at all the little nuggets of wisdom and joy to be gleamed\nfrom these pages. If I were someone who marked up books (which I would never,\nthe sacrilege!), I suspect I would've used up an entire highlighter trying to\ncapture all the worthy lines.  \n  \nI mentioned this wasn't a perfect read for me because while I particularly\nloved the beginning and the end, I did feel the middle sagged a bit. At times,\nit was a little overly long and meandering, with dialogue that was a bit too\nclever and may be verging on glib. And yet, the story continued to draw me in,\neven if the pacing wasn't quite even throughout.  \n  \nI will say, I think this is a story best enjoyed with a reader's full and\nundivided attention. Often it feels like there were a multitude of things\ngoing on at the same time\u2014the rapid-fire dialogue on the surface, the emotions\nunderneath, and the wisdom to be sussed out if looked carefully.\nUnfortunately, it's hard for me to find solid chunks of uninterrupted reading\ntime nowadays, so the book did lose some of its potency for me read in bits\nand pieces, a few lines at a time, especially in the middle there. But that's\nreally my bad and not the story's fault.  \n  \nI feel like the most resonant books are always the ones that address the human\nspirit\u2014its unfailing desire to endure, to belong, to love and be loved, and to\nfind meaning. And if you can sprinkle in a dash of humor along the way, all\nthe better. Well, if that's the case, then one cannot ask for a better story\nthan this.  \n  \n~~~~~~~~~~~~  \n\u2727 _Connect with me_ \u2727 _[Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/missreadaroo/) _\u2727  \n____________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________  \n\n[favorites](https://www.goodreads.com/review/list/30181442?shelf=favorites)\n[review-copy](https://www.goodreads.com/review/list/30181442?shelf=review-\ncopy)\n\n2,248 likes\n\n213 comments\n\n __Like\n\n __Comment\n\n __\n\n* * *\n\n[![Profile Image for Nilufer Ozmekik.](https://i.gr-\nassets.com/images/S/compressed.photo.goodreads.com/users/1718486814i/53701594._UY200_CR20,0,200,200_.jpg)](https://www.goodreads.com/user/show/53701594-nilufer-\nozmekik)\n\n[Nilufer Ozmekik](https://www.goodreads.com/user/show/53701594-nilufer-\nozmekik)\n\n3,261 reviews64.4k followers\n\nFollow\n\nFollow\n\n[August 19, 2024](https://www.goodreads.com/review/show/6768491180)\n\nWords cannot do justice to how brilliant this book is.
