Title: Jorge Espat
URL: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jorge_Espat
Excerpt: Jorge Luis Espat (born September 10, 1959) is a Belizean academic and politician. Espat is a former member People's United Party area representative in the Belize House of Representatives. He served on the faculty of St. John's College in Belize City as an instructor and later as president.
