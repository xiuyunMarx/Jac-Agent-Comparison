Title: Baroness (band)
URL: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baroness_(band)
Excerpt: Baroness is an American heavy metal band from Savannah, Georgia that has been active since 2003. Its early members grew up together in Lexington, Virginia. The band has undergone numerous lineup changes, with frontman John Baizley serving as the sole constant throughout. Baizley creates the artwork for all of the group's albums and has done artwork for other bands.

Title: Red Album (Baroness album)
URL: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_Album_(Baroness_album)
Excerpt: Red Album is the debut studio album by American heavy metal band Baroness. It was released in 2007 by Relapse Records.

Title: Yellow & Green (Baroness album)
URL: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellow_%26_Green_(Baroness_album)

Title: Purple (Baroness album)
URL: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purple_(Baroness_album)
Excerpt: Purple is the fourth studio album by American heavy metal band Baroness. It was released in 2015 through the band's record label, Abraxan Hymns.

Title: Stone (Baroness album)
URL: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stone_(Baroness_album)
Excerpt: Stone is the sixth studio album by American heavy metal band Baroness. The album was released on September 15, 2023, through the band's independent label Abraxan Hymns and was self-produced by the band.
