Rationale: The claim has two material parts that are contradicted by the evidence. Per the decision rule, a Wikipedia article about an entity that lists its roles and does not mention a claimed role is evidence against that part. Additionally, the Petals on the Wind (film) article lists the sequel's cast (Heather Graham, Rose McIver, Wyatt Nash, Bailey Buntain, Ellen Burstyn) with no mention of Shipka. Since two material parts of the claim are contradicted, the claim is NOT_SUPPORTED.
