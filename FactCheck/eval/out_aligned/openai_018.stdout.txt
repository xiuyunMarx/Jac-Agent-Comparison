Verdict: NOT_SUPPORTED
Rationale: The claim's central entity, the 'Inglis Barracks bombing,' is not supported by any evidence. The Wikipedia article on Inglis Barracks describes it only as 'a military installation in Mill Hill, London, NW7' with no mention of a bombing or IRA attack. Per the decision rule, a retrieved Wikipedia article about an entity that does not mention a claimed attribute is evidence against that part. Multiple searches for 'Inglis Barracks bombing,' 'Mill Hill barracks bombing,' and related terms returned no results. While the Brighton Hotel bombing is well-documented as an IRA attack in England on 12 October 1984 aimed at assassinating Prime Minister Margaret Thatcher, the claim that an 'Inglis Barracks bombing' was the first IRA attack in England since that event cannot be sustained because no evidence establishes that such a bombing occurred. Additionally, the Deal barracks bombing of September 1989 is documented as another IRA attack in England, which would further undermine any 'first since Brighton' claim if the Inglis Barracks bombing were later than that.
