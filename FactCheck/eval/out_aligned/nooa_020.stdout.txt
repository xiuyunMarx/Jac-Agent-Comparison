Verdict: NOT_SUPPORTED
Rationale: The claim contains two material errors. First, the singer's name is 'John Dyer Baizley,' not 'John Dyer Bailey' as stated in the claim. The Wikipedia article on John Dyer Baizley confirms he is the lead vocalist and rhythm guitarist of Baroness, formed in 2003 in Savannah, Georgia. Second, the claim states that baseball player Cy Twombly 'died in a car accident.' The Wikipedia article on Cy Twombly (baseball) states that Edwin Parker 'Cy' Twombly Sr. 'died at age 77 in Savannah, Georgia,' with no mention of a car accident. Multiple targeted searches for the cause of death returned no evidence of a car accident. Per the decision rule, a Wikipedia article that describes an entity's death without mentioning a claimed cause is evidence against that claim, not missing evidence. The remaining parts of the claim are supported: Baroness is indeed from Savannah, Georgia (the town where Cy Twombly died), and Gina Gleason is confirmed as the current lead guitarist of Baroness. However, the wrong name for the singer and the unsupported car-accident cause of death are material contradictions.
