Verdict: NOT_SUPPORTED
Rationale: The claim conflates multiple unrelated facts and contains material contradictions. (1) Detail (record producer) is indeed best known for producing "Drunk in Love" by Beyoncé, confirmed by multiple sources. However, (2) there is no evidence that any "Singapore freestyle" was referenced in "Drunk in Love" — the music video was shot in Golden Beach, Florida, and no source mentions any connection to Singapore or Singaporean swimming. (3) The claim states "They were undefeated since the Athens games with 28 medals." David Lim, a Singaporean freestyle swimmer, did win 28 medals, but these were at the Southeast Asian Games from 1981 to 1991, not at the Olympics. Singapore at the 2004 Athens Olympics won zero medals, directly contradicting the assertion of being "undefeated since the Athens games with 28 medals." Singapore's total Olympic medal count across all Games is only six (one gold, two silver, three bronze), far fewer than 28. David Lim himself competed at the 1984 and 1988 Olympics, not the 2004 Athens Games. The claim's material parts — the reference to Singapore freestyle in "Drunk in Love," and the assertion of being undefeated since Athens with 28 Olympic medals — are either unsupported or directly contradicted by the evidence.
