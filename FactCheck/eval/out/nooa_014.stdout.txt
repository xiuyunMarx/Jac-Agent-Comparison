Verdict: NOT_SUPPORTED
Rationale: The claim contains a material contradiction regarding the inauguration location of the SportAccord World Mind Games. Evidence from the SportAccord World Mind Games article states that 'The International Mind Sports Association (IMSA) inaugurated the SportAccord World Mind Games December 2011 in Beijing,' and that 'The first four meets, 2011 to 2014, were all held in Beijing during December.' This directly contradicts the claim's assertion that the games were inaugurated in Melbourne. Additionally, no evidence was found that Lubabalo Kondlo won a silver medal at the 2012 SportAccord World Mind Games. Multiple searches for Kondlo's participation, results, and medals at this event returned no relevant information. His Wikipedia article describes him as a South African English draughts grandmaster and former world champion in the GAYP version, with no mention of the 2012 SportAccord World Mind Games or any silver medal. Notably, Sergio Scarpetta is documented as winning the silver medal in checkers at the SportAccord World Mind Games in Beijing in December 2014 (not 2012), and at the separate 2012 World Mind Sports Games in Lille, France. The inauguration location is contradicted, and the silver medal claim is unsupported.
