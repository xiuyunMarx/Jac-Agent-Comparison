Title: List of Chinese by net worth
URL: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Chinese_by_net_worth
Summary: The following are partial lists of the Forbes list of Chinese billionaires (converted into USD) and is based on an annual assessment of wealth and assets compiled and published by Forbes magazine in 2022. In 2022, China had 607 billionaires which put the country second in the world, after the United States (735).
Current richest Chinese billionaire is Zhong Shanshan, ranked as the eighth wealthiest man in the world as of March 11, 2022.
