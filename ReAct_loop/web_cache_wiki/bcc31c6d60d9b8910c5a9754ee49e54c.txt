Title: Goodreads Choice Awards
URL: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goodreads_Choice_Awards
Summary: The Goodreads Choice Awards is a yearly award program, first launched on Goodreads in 2009.
Winners are determined by users voting on books that Goodreads has nominated or books of their choosing, released in the given year. Most books that Goodreads nominates are from verified Goodreads authors.  The final voting round collects the top 10 books from 20 different categories.

Title: R. F. Kuang
URL: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R._F._Kuang
Summary: Rebecca F. Kuang (Chinese: 匡灵秀; pinyin: Kuāng Língxiù; born May 29, 1996), writing as R. F. Kuang, is an American novelist and short-story writer. She is known for her fantasy novels, which include The Poppy War trilogy and her novel Babel, or the Necessity of Violence (2022), which placed first on The New York Times Best Seller list and won the 2022 Nebula Award for Best Novel. She is currently the  inaugural Millard Distinguished Writer in Residence at the College of the Holy Cross.

Title: Emily Henry
URL: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emily_Henry
Summary: Emily Henry (born May 17, 1991) is an American author who is best known for her 2020s romance novels Beach Read, People We Meet on Vacation, Book Lovers, Happy Place, Funny Story, and Great Big Beautiful Life, all of which are New York Times bestsellers.

Title: Travis Baldree
URL: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Travis_Baldree
Summary: Travis Baldree (born 1977) is an American author, audiobook narrator, and video game designer. He is best known for the video game Torchlight (2009), for narrating the audiobooks of the Cradle series by Will Wight, and for his novel Legends & Lattes (2022), which won the Astounding Award for Best New Writer.

Title: Rebecca Yarros
URL: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rebecca_Yarros
Summary: Rebecca Yarros (born April 13, 1981) is an American author. She is best known for the Empyrean fantasy book series, which will be adapted into a television series with Amazon; Yarros will serve as a non-writing executive producer. Yarros graduated from Troy University, where she studied European history and English.
