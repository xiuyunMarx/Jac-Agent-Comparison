[kattwilliamslive.com](https://kattwilliamslive.com)  \n  \n**Micah Sierra** \"**Katt** \" **Williams** (born September 2, 1971) is an\nAmerican stand-up comedian and actor. He played Money Mike in _[Friday After\nNext](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Friday_After_Next \"Friday After Next\")_ ,\nwas a recurring cast member on the first four seasons of _[Wild 'n\nOut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wild_'n_Out \"Wild 'n Out\")_ , portrayed\nBobby Shaw in _[My Wife and\nKids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/My_Wife_and_Kids \"My Wife and Kids\")_ ,\nprovided the voice of A Pimp Named Slickback in _[The\nBoondocks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Boondocks_\\(2005_TV_series\\) \"The\nBoondocks \\(2005 TV series\\)\")_ and Seamus in _[Cats& Dogs: The Revenge of\nKitty\nGalore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cats_&_Dogs:_The_Revenge_of_Kitty_Galore\n\"Cats & Dogs: The Revenge of Kitty Galore\")_, and portrayed Lord Have Mercy in\n_[Norbit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norbit \"Norbit\")_.\n\n## Early life\n\n[[edit](/w/index.php?title=Katt_Williams&action=edit&section=1 \"Edit section:\nEarly life\")]\n\nMicah Williams was born in [Cincinnati,\nOhio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cincinnati \"Cincinnati\"), on September 2,\n1971.[1][2][3] He was raised in [Dayton,\nOhio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dayton,_Ohio \"Dayton, Ohio\") by [Jehovah's\nWitness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jehovah's_Witness \"Jehovah's Witness\")\nparents.[4][3] He stated that he learned to read at three years old, and was\ncommunicating in multiple languages, including French and [Haitian\nCreole](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haitian_Creole \"Haitian Creole\"). During\nhis childhood, he lived in [Haiti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haiti\n\"Haiti\") for a year and a half on religious mission trips with his family.[5]\n\nWilliams [emancipated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emancipation_of_minors\n\"Emancipation of minors\") himself from his parents at age 13 and moved to\nFlorida. Though homeless and living in a park, he supported himself as a\nstreet vendor.[6][7] He stated that he wanted to remain God's friend.[4]\n\nSince at least 2016, Williams has stated on numerous occasions that he\nenlisted in the [United States Marine\nCorps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Marine_Corps \"United States\nMarine Corps\") as a minor at the age of 16, attended boot camp, and was\nsubsequently discharged when his age was discovered. However, in a CBS News\ninvestigation, the Marine Corps stated that there was no record of Williams\nhaving ever served in the military or attended any Marine training\nfacilities.[8]\n\n## Career\n\n[[edit](/w/index.php?title=Katt_Williams&action=edit&section=2 \"Edit section:\nCareer\")]\n\n### Stand-up comedy\n\n[[edit](/w/index.php?title=Katt_Williams&action=edit&section=3 \"Edit section:\nStand-up comedy\")]\n\nWilliams started performing comedy in the\n[Avondale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avondale,_Cincinnati \"Avondale,\nCincinnati\") neighborhood of Cincinnati.[9][10] He honed his comic delivery by\nperforming his routine in clubs around the country and had become an\nestablished comic by 1999, appearing at the likes of [The\nImprov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Improv \"The Improv\"), The Comedy\nClub, [The Ice\nHouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Ice_House_\\(comedy_club\\) \"The Ice\nHouse \\(comedy club\\)\"), and [Hollywood Park\nCasino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hollywood_Park_Casino \"Hollywood Park\nCasino\"). Most notably, he appeared on [BET](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BET\n\"BET\")'s _[ComicView](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ComicView \"ComicView\")_ as\nKatt \"N da Hatt\" Williams.[9][10]\n\nWilliams starred in his first comedy special, _Let a Playa Play_ , in 2006.\nHis first [HBO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HBO \"HBO\") stand-up special came\nin 2006 with _The Pimp Chronicles, Pt. 1_.[9] In 2007, he co-wrote and starred\nas himself in the comedy film _[Katt Williams: American\nHustle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katt_Williams:_American_Hustle \"Katt\nWilliams: American Hustle\")_. The film had critical success and established\nWilliams as a mainstream comedian.[9][11] In 2008, he released his second\n[HBO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HBO \"HBO\") comedy special, _[It's Pimpin'\nPimpin'](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/It's_Pimpin'_Pimpin' \"It's Pimpin'\nPimpin'\")_.[12][13] Keeping busy releasing comedy DVDs and touring, Williams\nhad a comedy tour that was named the best of 2008 by _Billboard_.[13]\n\nAfter a four-year hiatus, Williams returned to stand-up in 2012 for his third\nHBO comedy special, _Kattpacalypse_.[14] Later that year, he ended up in jail\nafter an incident at a [Seattle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seattle\n\"Seattle\") bar where he reportedly acted threatening and violent, then\nresisted arrest. The next day, Williams announced the end of his stand-up\ncomedy career.[15] However, three days later, he announced he would not\nretire.[16]\n\nTwo live performances in November 2012 ended early because of Williams'\nconfrontational behavior. A November 1 performance at the [Wells Fargo\nTheatre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wells_Fargo_Theatre \"Wells Fargo\nTheatre\") in [Denver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denver \"Denver\") ended\nafter he jumped off the stage to confront a\n[heckler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heckler \"Heckler\").[17] Williams'\nNovember 16 performance at the [Oracle\nArena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oracle_Arena \"Oracle Arena\") in\n[Oakland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oakland \"Oakland\") ended after he\nengaged in a profanity-laced confrontation with a heckler, and was assisted\noff stage by his own security team.[18]\n\nDuring late 2013, Williams was on his Growth Spurt Tour.[19] On August 16,\n2014, he returned with a new HBO special titled _Katt Williams: Priceless:\nAfterlife_ , which was directed by [Spike\nLee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spike_Lee \"Spike Lee\").[20][21]\n\nIn September 2015, during an interview, Williams announced and described his\nupcoming Conspiracy Theory Tour: \"The [conspiracy\nconversation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conspiracy_theory \"Conspiracy\ntheory\") is a conversation that we are all familiar with. We know that there\nare conspiracies out there, but this is a conversation that encompasses a lot\nof things that aren't being discussed other places. That's the basis for all\nconspiracy theories: the fact that there is hidden information out there, and\nhow our process changes about things that we thought we used to know. We all,\nat some point, if we're are at a certain age, we grew up [thinking Pluto was a\nplanet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pluto#IAU_classification \"Pluto\"). This\nis probably going to go down as one of my finest works, just because it's a\ncollection of forbidden topics that we can't seem to get answered. I am one of\nthe rare urban public officials. Part of my guarantee in my ticket price is\nthat I'm going to be talking about what we are talking about now, and\ndiscussing from now to the next time we see [me] again. This is the open\ndiscussion that we've had since 2003. This is what it is about.\"[22]\n\nIn 2018, Williams released a new stand-up special on\n[Netflix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netflix \"Netflix\") called _Great\nAmerica_ and shot in [Jacksonville,\nFlorida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacksonville,_Florida \"Jacksonville,\nFlorida\").[23]\n\nIn 2022, he released a new stand-up special on Netflix called _World War III_.\n\nIn January 2024, Williams was featured on retired NFL player [Shannon\nSharpe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shannon_Sharpe \"Shannon Sharpe\")'s\npodcast, _[Club Shay Shay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Club_Shay_Shay \"Club\nShay Shay\")_. Williams' comments on the entertainment industry and other\ncomedians including [Cedric the\nEntertainer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cedric_the_Entertainer \"Cedric the\nEntertainer\"), [Steve Harvey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Harvey\n\"Steve Harvey\"), and [Rickey\nSmiley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rickey_Smiley \"Rickey Smiley\") gained\ntraction on social media.[24]\n\nOn May 4, 2024, _Katt Williams: Woke Foke_ streamed live from Los Angeles.\nPart of _[Netflix Is a Joke\nFestival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netflix_Is_a_Joke_Festival \"Netflix Is\na Joke Festival\")_ , it was Williams' thirteenth stand-up comedy special (his\nthird for\n[Netflix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Netflix_original_stand-\nup_comedy_specials \"List of Netflix original stand-up comedy specials\")).[25]\n\n### Acting\n\n[[edit](/w/index.php?title=Katt_Williams&action=edit&section=4 \"Edit section:\nActing\")]\n\nIn 2002, Williams made his acting debut on _[NYPD\nBlue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NYPD_Blue \"NYPD Blue\")_ on the sixth\nepisode of its tenth season on October 29. He has appeared in several episodes of _[My Wife\nand Kids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/My_Wife_and_Kids \"My Wife and Kids\")_\nas character Bobby Shaw.
