Le\u00f3n](https://www.cnbc.com/riley-de-\nleon/)[@RileyCNBC](https://twitter.com/RileyCNBC)\n\n[WATCH LIVE](/live-tv/)\n\nKey Points\n\n  * Lineage closed up more than 3% during its market debut Thursday on the Nasdaq after going public under the ticker symbol \u201cLINE.\u201d\n  * The company raised $4.4 billion at an implied valuation over $18 billion, making it the largest public offering since chip designer Arm's $4.8 billion listing last September.\n  * The company is the world\u2019s largest global temperature-controlled warehouse REIT with 480 locations spanning North America, Nasdaq in biggest IPO of the year\n\n[Halftime Report](https://www.cnbc.com/halftime/)\n\nLineage, the largest temperature-controlled warehouse real estate investment\ntrust (REIT) in the world, closed up more than 3% in its Nasdaq Stock Market\ndebut on Thursday, after going public [under the ticker symbol\n\"LINE.\"](https://www.cnbc.com/quotes/LINE)\n\nLineage priced 57 million shares at $78 apiece on Wednesday, near the top of\nits initial $70 to $82 target range. The company raised $4.4 billion at an\nimplied valuation over $18 billion, making it the largest public offering\nsince chip designer [Arm](/quotes/ARM/)'s $4.8 billion listing last September\nand more than twice the size of cruise operator [Viking\nHoldings](https://www.cnbc.com/quotes/VIK/), which went public in May.\n\nLineage is a four-time [CNBC Disruptor 50](http://cnbc.com/disruptors) company\nthat [ranked No. 46](https://www.cnbc.com/2024/05/14/lineage-cnbc-\ndisruptor-50.html) on this year's list.\n\n## More coverage of the 2024 CNBC Disruptor 50\n\n  * [These are the 2024 CNBC Disruptor 50 companies](https://www.cnbc.com/2024/05/14/these-are-the-2024-cnbc-disruptor-50-companies.html)\n  * [How we chose the 2024 CNBC Disruptor 50](https://www.cnbc.com/2024/05/14/the-2024-cnbc-disruptor-50-how-we-chose-the-companies.html)\n  * [Why OpenAI is the first company to top the CNBC Disruptor 50 list for two years in a row](https://www.cnbc.com/2024/05/14/openai-is-first-company-to-be-no-1-on-disruptor-50-two-years-in-a-row.html)\n\nOne of the biggest factors in the company's success is its aggressive\nacquisition strategy.\n\n\"We started with one warehouse and we've done 116 acquisitions to turn Lineage\ninto what it is today,\" co-founder and co-executive chairman Adam Forste said\non CNBC's \"[Squawk Box](https://www.cnbc.com/squawk-box-us/)\" Thursday morning\nbefore shares started trading.\n\nIn the last year alone, Lineage has acquired Grupo Fuentes, Burris Logistics,\nKennedy Transportation and Harnes. The Burris acquisition alone gave Lineage\n[eight new facilities.](https://www.onelineage.com/news-stories/lineage-\nacquires-warehousing-and-e-commerce-assets-burris-logistics)\n\n\"So many families who we've bought companies from rolled equity into Lineage\nas part of their transaction, so they're celebrating here today with us as\nwell,\" Forste said, adding that the company's namesake is derived from the\nnetwork, or lineage, of family-owned warehouses that he and co-founder Kevin\nMarchetti have brought into the fold.\n\nLineage has amassed over 480 facilities, totaling roughly [2.9 billion cubic\nfeet of capacity](https://www.onelineage.com/news-stories/lineage-enters-\nadditional-strategic-us-market-new-facility-near-\nphoenix#:~:text=Lineage%20is%20one%20of%20the,Europe%2C%20and%20Asia%2DPacific.)\nacross countries in North America, Europe, and the Asia-Pacific region. These\nlocations create a global network of cold-storage facilities, reducing supply\nchain food waste and its environmental impact.\n\nFood loss happens at every level of the supply chain, with an estimated $600\nbillion worth of food [going to waste during or just after\nharvest.](https://www.mckinsey.com/industries/consumer-packaged-goods/our-\ninsights/reducing-food-loss-what-grocery-retailers-and-manufacturers-can-do)\nThat ever-growing amount of waste currently accounts for about 11% of the\nworld's emissions \u2013 making it one of the biggest environmental issues\ncontributing to climate change.\n\nMorgan Stanley, Goldman Sachs, Bank of America, J.P. Morgan and Wells Fargo\nwere the lead underwriters for Lineage's offering.\n\n[Sign up](https://www.cnbc.com/disruptorsnewsletter/) for our weekly, original\nnewsletter that goes beyond the annual
