As of December 2025, Bangkok has 204 completed\n[skyscrapers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skyscraper \"Skyscraper\") over 150\nmeters (492 feet) in height, of which 129 are listed on the Council on Tall\nBuildings and Urban Habitat database.[1]\n\nBangkok experienced a building boom in the late 1980s and early 1990s when\nThailand experienced rapid economic growth. However, the [1997 Asian Financial\nCrisis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1997_Asian_financial_crisis \"1997 Asian\nfinancial crisis\") left a visible scar on the city's skyline with many\nunfinished or abandoned buildings. The [Thai\neconomy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economy_of_Thailand \"Economy of\nThailand\") rebounded in the early 2000s and many projects resumed\nconstruction. A new wave of building boom starting from the 2010s and\ncontinuing up to the present day was spurred by the development of the city's\n[mass rapid transit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rapid_transit \"Rapid\ntransit\") systems, with new development concentrated around\n[MRT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MRT_\\(Bangkok\\) \"MRT \\(Bangkok\\)\") and\n[BTS skytrain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BTS_Skytrain \"BTS Skytrain\")\nstations.\n\nMagnolias Waterfront Residences at\n[Iconsiam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iconsiam \"Iconsiam\"), completed in\n2018, is Bangkok's tallest building at 318 metres (1,043 feet), followed by\n[King Power Mahanakhon\nTower](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_Power_Mahanakhon \"King Power\nMahanakhon\") at 314 metres (1,034 feet). Several skyscraper projects are\ncurrently under construction or in the planning phase, including the 92-storey\n437 metres/1,435 feet tall Signature Tower at [One\nBangkok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One_Bangkok \"One Bangkok\") complex,\n[Dusit Thani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dusit_Thani_Bangkok \"Dusit Thani\nBangkok\") Residences and Asiatique the Riverfront's Iconic Tower. The latter\nis designed by [Adrian Smith and Gordon\nGill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adrian_Smith_+_Gordon_Gill_Architecture\n\"Adrian Smith + Gordon Gill Architecture\"),[2] architects of the [Burj\nKhalifa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burj_Khalifa \"Burj Khalifa\") in Dubai.\n\n[![](//thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/9/98/Bangkok_skyline_view_from_Mahanakhon_Tower.jpg/1920px-\nBangkok_skyline_view_from_Mahanakhon_Tower.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Bangkok_skyline_view_from_Mahanakhon_Tower.jpg)\n\nPanoramic view of Bangkok's skyline looking north/north-east from King Power\n[Mahanakhon Tower](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_Power_Mahanakhon \"King\nPower Mahanakhon\")\n\n## Tallest completed buildings\n\n[[edit](/w/index.php?title=List_of_tallest_buildings_in_Bangkok&action=edit&section=1\n\"Edit section: Tallest completed buildings\")]\n\nThis list ranks completed and topped out skyscrapers in Bangkok that stand at\nleast 150m/492 ft based on standard height measurement.
