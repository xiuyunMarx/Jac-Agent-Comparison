4th Provost (1810\u20131813), 3rd vice provost (1789\u20131810)[2]\n  * [Alexander Dallas Bache](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Dallas_Bache \"Alexander Dallas Bache\") (1806\u20131867): physicist, scientist, and surveyor, professor of natural philosophy and chemistry, superintendent of the United States Coast Survey\n  * [Edmund Bacon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edmund_Bacon_\\(architect\\) \"Edmund Bacon \\(architect\\)\"): adjunct professor of architecture\n  * [E. Digby Baltzell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E._Digby_Baltzell \"E. Digby Baltzell\"): emeritus professor of history and sociology; scholar and author; creator of the acronym \"[WASP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_Anglo-Saxon_Protestant \"White Anglo-Saxon Protestant\")\"\n  * [Aaron T. Beck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aaron_T._Beck \"Aaron T. Beck\"): emeritus professor of psychiatry; considered the father of both [cognitive therapy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cognitive_therapy \"Cognitive therapy\") and [cognitive behavioral therapy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cognitive_behavioral_therapy \"Cognitive behavioral therapy\")[3][4]\n  * [Richard Beeman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Beeman \"Richard Beeman\"): John Walsh Centennial Professor of History; [Fulbright Scholar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fulbright_Scholar \"Fulbright Scholar\")\n  * [Janice R. Bellace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Janice_R._Bellace \"Janice R. Bellace\"): deputy provost and director of the [Huntsman Program in International Studies and Business](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huntsman_Program_in_International_Studies_and_Business \"Huntsman Program in International Studies and Business\")\n  * [Charles Bernstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Bernstein_\\(poet\\) \"Charles Bernstein \\(poet\\)\"): Donald T. Regan Professor of English, prominent [language poet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Language_poets \"Language poets\")\n  * [Mary Frances Berry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mary_Frances_Berry \"Mary Frances Berry\"): Geraldine Segal Professor of Social Thought; former chair US Civil Rights Commission\n  * [Joseph R. Biden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_R._Biden \"Joseph R. Biden\"), former Benjamin Franklin Professor of Presidential Practice: 2017\u2013 April 2019 (presently on \"leave of absence\")[5] 46th [president of the United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_the_United_States \"President of the United States\")\n  * [Ray Birdwhistell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ray_Birdwhistell \"Ray Birdwhistell\"): professor, [Annenberg School for Communication at the University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annenberg_School_for_Communication_at_the_University_of_Pennsylvania \"Annenberg School for Communication at the University of Pennsylvania\")\n  * [Matt Blaze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_Blaze \"Matt Blaze\"): associate professor of computer science\n  * [Antony J. Blinken](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antony_J._Blinken \"Antony J. Blinken\"): [US secretary of state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Secretary_of_State \"United States Secretary of State\") under President [Joseph R. Biden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_R._Biden \"Joseph R. Biden\"), director of the [Penn Biden Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penn_Biden_Center \"Penn Biden Center\") 2018\u20132020\n  * [Sidney A. Bludman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sidney_A._Bludman \"Sidney A. Bludman\"): physicist\n  * [John Bowker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Bowker_\\(theologian\\) \"John Bowker \\(theologian\\)\"): theologian\n  * [Eric Bradlow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eric_Bradlow \"Eric Bradlow\"): K.P. Chao Professor, professor of marketing, statistics, education and economics\n  * [Ralph L. Brinster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ralph_L._Brinster \"Ralph L. Brinster\"): Richard King Mellon Professor of Reproductive Physiology, creator of the transgenic mouse; [National Medal of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Medal_of_Science \"National Medal of Science\") recipient\n  * [Lawton Burns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lawton_Burns \"Lawton Burns\"): chair of the Health Care Management Department of the Wharton School; James Joo-Jin Kim Professor\n  * [Eugenio Calabi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eugenio_Calabi \"Eugenio Calabi\"): [Thomas A. Scott Professor Emeritus of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_A._Scott_Professorship_of_Mathematics \"Thomas A. Scott Professorship of Mathematics\"), known for his development of the [Calabi\u2013Yau manifold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calabi\u2013Yau_manifold \"Calabi\u2013Yau manifold\")\n  * [Arthur Caplan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthur_Caplan \"Arthur Caplan\"): Emanuel and Robert Hart Professor of Bioethics\n  * [Britton Chance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Britton_Chance \"Britton Chance\"): National Medal of Science recipient; professor of biophysics\n  * [Roger Chartier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roger_Chartier \"Roger Chartier\"): professor of history; chair of history at the Coll\u00e8ge de France; leading cultural historian\n  * [Pei-yuan Chia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pei-yuan_Chia \"Pei-yuan Chia\"): senior fellow of the CSI Center for Advanced Studies in Management at the [Wharton School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wharton_School \"Wharton School\"); former vice chairman of [Citicorp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citicorp \"Citicorp\") and [Citibank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citibank \"Citibank\"), member of [AIG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_International_Group \"American International Group\")'s board of directors\n  * [Thomas Childers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Childers \"Thomas Childers\"): Sheldon and Lucy Hackney Professor of History; author of numerous history publications and recipient of teaching awards\n  * [Wallace H. Clark Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wallace_H._Clark_Jr. \"Wallace H. Clark Jr.\"): pathologist, cancer researcher\n  * [Mildred Cohn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mildred_Cohn \"Mildred Cohn\"): National Medal of Science recipient; professor of biophysics and physical biochemistry\n  * [George Crumb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Crumb \"George Crumb\"): [Pulitzer Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pulitzer_Prize \"Pulitzer Prize\") winner in music for \"Echoes of Time and the River\" in 1968 and received a [Grammy Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award \"Grammy Award\") for Best Classical Contemporary Composition for \"Star-Child\" in 2001; [Walter H. Annenberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walter_H._Annenberg \"Walter H. Annenberg\") Professor in the Humanities and Professor in Music Department at Penn (1965\u20131997)[6]\n  * [Raymond Davis Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raymond_Davis_Jr. \"Raymond Davis Jr.\"): National Medal of Science recipient; Nobel laureate; research professor of physics and astronomy\n  * [Emile B. De Sauz\u00e9](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emile_B._De_Sauz\u00e9 \"Emile B. De Sauz\u00e9\"): language educator known for developing the conversational method of learning a language\n  * [Frederick Dickinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frederick_Dickinson \"Frederick Dickinson\"): professor of Japanese history and co-director of the Lauder Institute of Management and International Studies\n  * [John DiIulio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_DiIulio"}
