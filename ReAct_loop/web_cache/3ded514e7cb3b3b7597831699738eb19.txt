ancient\ntemple in [Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rome \"Rome\"), Italy, originally\nbuilt under emperor [Augustus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Augustus\n\"Augustus\") (27 BC\u2013AD 14) and reconstructed under\n[Hadrian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hadrian \"Hadrian\") (117\u2013138); since AD\n609, it is a [Catholic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholic_Church\n\"Catholic Church\") church called the **Basilica of St. Mary and the Martyrs**\n([Italian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italian_language \"Italian language\"):\n_Basilica di Santa Maria ad Martyres_). It is perhaps the most famous, and\narchitecturally most influential,\n[rotunda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotunda_\\(architecture\\) \"Rotunda\n\\(architecture\\)\").\n\nThe current Pantheon was built on the site of an earlier temple, which had\nbeen commissioned by [Marcus Vipsanius\nAgrippa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marcus_Vipsanius_Agrippa \"Marcus\nVipsanius Agrippa\") during the reign of\n[Augustus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Augustus \"Augustus\"). After the\noriginal burnt down, the present building was completed by the\n[emperor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_emperor \"Roman emperor\")\n[Hadrian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hadrian \"Hadrian\") and probably\ndedicated c. AD 126. Its date of construction is uncertain, because Hadrian\nchose to re-inscribe the new temple with Agrippa's original date inscription\nfrom the older temple.\n\nThe building is round in plan, except for the\n[portico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portico \"Portico\") with large granite\n[Corinthian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corinthian_order \"Corinthian\norder\") columns (eight in the first rank and two groups of four behind) under\na [pediment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pediment \"Pediment\"). A rectangular\n[vestibule](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vestibule_\\(architecture\\)\n\"Vestibule \\(architecture\\)\") links the porch to the\n[rotunda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotunda_\\(architecture\\) \"Rotunda\n\\(architecture\\)\"), which is under a\n[coffered](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coffer \"Coffer\")\n[concrete](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concrete \"Concrete\")\n[dome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dome \"Dome\") made from Roman concrete\n(also called [opus\ncaementicium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Opus_caementicium \"Opus\ncaementicium\")), with a central opening\n([oculus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki"}
