[www.westreadingborough.com](http://www.westreadingborough.com)  \n  \n**West Reading** is a\n[borough](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borough_\\(Pennsylvania\\) \"Borough\n\\(Pennsylvania\\)\") in [Berks County,\nPennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berks_County,_Pennsylvania \"Berks\nCounty, Pennsylvania\"), United States, located next to the city of\n[Reading](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reading,_Pennsylvania \"Reading,\nPennsylvania\"). The population was 4,553 at the [2020\ncensus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_census \"2020 United\nStates census\"). It contains a vibrant main street (Penn Avenue) and the large\n[Reading Hospital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reading_Hospital \"Reading\nHospital\") and Medical Center. It was also the site of the [VF Outlet\nVillage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/VF_Corporation \"VF Corporation\"), one\nof the largest [outlet malls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outlet_store\n\"Outlet store\") in the United States. The VF Outlet Village was located in the\nbuildings of the former Berkshire Knitting Mills, which was in operation from\n1908 to 1975.[3] The VF Outlet closed in 2020.[4]\n\n## History\n\n[[edit](/w/index.php?title=West_Reading,_Pennsylvania&action=edit&section=1\n\"Edit section: History\")]\n\nThe [Lenape](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lenape \"Lenape\") tribe occupied the\narea.[5]\n\nThe borough was settled in 1873 and incorporated on March 18, 1907. It is settled on a hilly region, as usual for towns in\nthe [Piedmont (plateau)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piedmont_\\(plateau\\)\n\"Piedmont \\(plateau\\)\"). The previously mentioned Penn Avenue interchanges\nwith [U.S. Route 422](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Route_422 \"U.S. Route\n422\") going eastbound, along bordering two directions railways to the north\nand east.
