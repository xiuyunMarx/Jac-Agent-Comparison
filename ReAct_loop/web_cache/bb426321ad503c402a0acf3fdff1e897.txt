My name is Elana, and I\u2019m Kickstarter\u2019s VP of\nProduct and Design. Today I\u2019m writing to tell you about a new feature we\u2019ve\nlaunched that builds on other recent improvements we\u2019ve made to the platform\nas part of our commitment to keep creators and backers safe.\n\nCreators will sometimes report to us that they\u2019ve experienced harassment in\nthe comments on their project page or in their project updates. Because\ncomments on a project can only be left by backers of that project, we\u2019ve seen\nthat there are sometimes people who become backers (often making very small\npledges) only so they are able to leave abusive comments. Obviously, these are\nnot backers who are pledging to support a project or its creator in good\nfaith, and creators (as well as other backers) have let us know that they\nwould like us to do something about it.\n\nOur solution is a new feature that enables creators to flag potentially\nabusive backers to our Trust & Safety team for review. Using this feature, a\ncreator can quickly and easily let us know about a backer they believe is\nharassing them. Comments from this backer will be immediately hidden from\npublic view while our team reviews the creator\u2019s request and determines\nwhether the backer has indeed violated our [Community\nGuidelines](https://www.kickstarter.com/help/community) and should be\npermanently removed.\n\nIt\u2019s important to know that flagging a backer through this request does not\nguarantee that they will be removed from a project. Instead, it alerts us to a\nsituation that a creator believes we should look into, and enables us to\nquickly act on it. There are of course times when a backer might leave a\nlegitimate critical comment that a creator doesn\u2019t like\u2014comments that do not\nviolate our guidelines will not cause a backer to be removed from a project.\n\nTo submit a request, a creator will need to select the comment that they\nbelieve to be abusive and click on the backer\u2019s name. They\u2019ll see a \u201cRemove\nbacker\u201d button in the modal that opens up which they can click to start the\nrequest. The creator will then need to write a description (at least 140\ncharacters) of why they believe the backer should be removed as well as select\nthe appropriate category that aligns with their explanation. This information\nwill help give us the context we need in order to investigate and make a\ndecision.\n\nIf our Trust & Safety team agrees that the backer has violated our guidelines,\nwe\u2019ll email the creator a confirmation and remove the backer from the project.\nWe\u2019ll also send the backer an email alerting them to the fact that they have\nbeen removed from the project and are no longer able to back it\u2014the backer\nwill also see this information in a banner at the top of Kickstarter\u2019s site\nwhen they are logged in.\n\nIf our team decides that the backer didn\u2019t violate our guidelines, the creator\nwill receive an email with this update, and the backer\u2019s comments will be made\nvisible once again. In cases like this, we will not notify the backer about\nthe request\u2014they will only be alerted if we ultimately determine that they\nhave violated our guidelines.\n\nI want to send a big thank you to all the creators and backers who have\ngenerously given their time to talk to us behind the scenes about new features\nlike this, and who have provided valuable insight into what they\u2019d like to see\nfrom us. On that note, if you haven\u2019t already [read\nup](https://www.kickstarter.com/blog/reporting-back-from-the-first-meeting-of-\nthe-kickstarter-comm) on the work we\u2019re doing with the [Kickstarter Community\nAdvisory Council](https://www.kickstarter.com/articles/kickstarter-community-\nadvisory-council), please do! This [amazing\ngroup](https://www.kickstarter.com/blog/welcome-to-the-members-of-\nkickstarters-community-advisory-cou), which launched in June, is made up of a\ndiverse group of Kickstarter creators from across a wide range of creative\nindustries. We\u2019re thrilled to be collaborating with them to make Kickstarter\nas useful, welcoming, and inclusive as it can possibly be.\n\n_\u2014Elana Jacobs; VP, Product and Design_\n\n**Read about other safety improvements we\u2019ve recently made to Kickstarter:**\n\n[Improving Comment Moderation\u2014Part of Our Commitment to Keeping Creators and\nBackers Safe](https://www.kickstarter.com/blog/improving-comment-\nmoderationpart-of-our-commitment-to-keeping-0)\n\n[More Commenting Improvements Designed to Keep Creators and Backers Safe\n](https://www.kickstarter.com/blog/more-commenting-improvements-designed-to-\nkeep-creator"}
