\"List of Dallas Cowboys seasons\")**\n  * [2025](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Dallas_Cowboys_season \"2025 Dallas Cowboys season\") \u2192\n\n  \n  \nThe [2024 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_NFL_season \"2024 NFL\nseason\") was the [Dallas Cowboys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dallas_Cowboys\n\"Dallas Cowboys\")' 65th in the [National Football\nLeague](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Football_League \"National\nFootball League\") (NFL) and their fifth and final season under [head\ncoach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Dallas_Cowboys_head_coaches \"List\nof Dallas Cowboys head coaches\") [Mike\nMcCarthy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_McCarthy \"Mike McCarthy\"). This\nwas their first season since\n[2020](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Dallas_Cowboys_season \"2020 Dallas\nCowboys season\") without [Dan\nQuinn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dan_Quinn_\\(American_football\\) \"Dan\nQuinn \\(American football\\)\") as defensive coordinator, as the [Washington\nCommanders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington_Commanders \"Washington\nCommanders\") hired him to be their head coach. He was replaced in that role by\nformer [Minnesota Vikings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minnesota_Vikings\n\"Minnesota Vikings\") head coach [Mike\nZimmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Zimmer \"Mike Zimmer\"), who returned\nto the Cowboys after eighteen years. The Cowboys failed to improve upon their\n12\u20135 record from the past three seasons following a loss to the [Atlanta\nFalcons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Atlanta_Falcons_season \"2024\nAtlanta Falcons season\") in Week 9, and failed to match it after a loss to the\n[Philadelphia\nEagles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Philadelphia_Eagles_season \"2024\nPhiladelphia Eagles season\") in Week 10. After the Eagles defeated the\n[Carolina\nPanthers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Carolina_Panthers_season \"2024\nCarolina Panthers season\") in Week 14, the Cowboys were knocked out of NFC\nEast contention, marking the 20th straight season the division would not have\na repeat champion. With the Commanders' 36\u201333 win over the Eagles in Week 16,\nthe Cowboys were eliminated from playoff contention for the first time since\n[2020](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Dallas_Cowboys_season \"2020 Dallas\nCowboys season\").[1] After their 41\u20137 loss at Philadelphia in Week 17, the\nCowboys suffered a losing season for the first time since that same year, and\nwere also swept by the Eagles for the first time since\n[2011](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_Dallas_Cowboys_season \"2011 Dallas\nCowboys season\").\n\nFor the first time since 2010, offensive tackle [Tyron\nSmith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tyron_Smith \"Tyron Smith\") was not on the\nroster, as he signed with the [New York\nJets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_New_York_Jets_season \"2024 New York\nJets season\") during free agency.[2] On April 29, running back [Ezekiel\nElliott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ezekiel_Elliott \"Ezekiel Elliott\")\nsigned a one-year contract to return to the team after a year away.[3]\n\nThe Cowboys notably struggled at home, going 2\u20137 at home. Conversely, they\nperformed moderately well on the road with a 5\u20133 road record. The 2\u20137 home\nrecord notably came after a year in which the Cowboys went undefeated at home\nin the regular season at 8\u20130.\n\nAfter the season, head coach Mike McCarthy's contract expired. It was\nannounced McCarthy would not return to the Cowboys, as the two sides failed to\nreach an extension, ending McCarthy's tenure in Dallas.[4] Offensive\nCoordinator Brian Schottenheimer was promoted to head to coach on January 24,\n2025.[5]\n\n## Offseason\n\n[[edit](/w/index.php?title=2024_Dallas_Cowboys_season&action=edit&section=1\n\"Edit section: Offseason\")]\n\n### Signings\n\n[[edit](/w/index.php?title=2024_Dallas_Cowboys_season&action=edit&section=2\n\"Edit section: Signings\")]\n\nPosition | Player | Age | 2023 team | Contract  \n---|---|---|---|---  \nRB| [Ezekiel Elliott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ezekiel_Elliott \"Ezekiel\nElliott\")| 29| [New England\nPatriots](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_New_England_Patriots_season \"2023\nNew England Patriots season\")| 1 year, $3 million  \nRB| [Royce Freeman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royce_Freeman \"Royce\nFreeman\")| 28| [Los Angeles\nRams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Los_Angeles_Rams_season \"2023 Los\nAngeles Rams season\")| 1 year, $1.25 million  \nLB| [Eric Kendricks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eric_Kendricks \"Eric\nKendricks\")| 32| [Los Angeles\nChargers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Los_Angeles_Chargers_season \"2023\nLos Angeles Chargers season\")| 1 year, $3 million  \n  \n### Re-signings\n\n[[edit](/w/index.php?title=2024_Dallas_Cowboys_season&action=edit&section=3\n\"Edit section: Re-signings\")]\n\nPosition | Player | Age | Contract  \n---|---|---|---  \nRB| [Rico Dowdle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rico_Dowdle \"Rico Dowdle\")|\n25| 1 year, $1.25 million  \nG| [Chuma Edoga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chuma_Edoga \"Chuma Edoga\")| 27|\n1 year, $1.3 million  \nCB| [C.
