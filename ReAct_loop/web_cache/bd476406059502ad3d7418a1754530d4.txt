visited\ncities\"))\n\nThis is a **list of the top 100 cities ranked by the number of international\nvisitors** , including all international arrivals by land, air, and sea, for\ntourist or business purposes. The [consulting\nfirm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Management_consulting \"Management\nconsulting\") [Euromonitor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euromonitor\n\"Euromonitor\") and the [financial\nservices](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Financial_services \"Financial\nservices\") corporation [Mastercard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mastercard\n\"Mastercard\") define the concept of the foreign visitor differently, thus\ntheir respective rankings differ.\n\nEuromonitor counts a visitor as any person visiting a city in another country\nfor at least 24 hours, for a period not exceeding 12 months, and staying in\npaid or unpaid, collective or private accommodation. Each arrival is counted\nseparately and includes people traveling more than once a year and people\nvisiting several cities during one trip. The growth column compares\ninternational arrivals to the previous year.[1][2]\n\nMastercard Global Destinations Cities Index counts a visitor only if a person\nstayed overnight at least once in the city. The income column shows the amount\nvisitors spent in each city. For cities bordering directly on foreign\nterritory, border crossings from country to country are not counted as\ninternational visitors.[3]\n\n## 2025 top 10 ranking\n\nThe top 10 most visited cities
