frog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anomaloglossus_beebei\n\"Anomaloglossus beebei\"), Kaieteur National Park.\n\n**Kaieteur National Park** is a [national\npark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_park \"National park\") located in\nthe [Potaro-Siparuni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Potaro-Siparuni \"Potaro-\nSiparuni\") Region of [Guyana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guyana \"Guyana\"),\nroughly 633 km (393.33 miles) south of\n[Georgetown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgetown,_Guyana \"Georgetown,\nGuyana\"), the nation's capital, closer to the border with\n[Brazil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazil \"Brazil\") than to the\n[Caribbean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caribbean_Sea \"Caribbean Sea\")\ncoast. It is widely considered the country's only national park,[1][2] as the\ncapital's [National\nPark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Park_\\(Georgetown\\) \"National\nPark \\(Georgetown\\)\") is a not a true [wilderness\nreserve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nature_reserve \"Nature reserve\").\nKaieteur is part of the [Guianan moist\nforests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guianan_moist_forests \"Guianan moist\nforests\") ecoregion.[3] The main tourist attraction in the park is [Kaieteur\nFalls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaieteur_Falls \"Kaieteur Falls\"),\nconsidered the largest single-drop\n[waterfall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waterfall \"Waterfall\") anywhere on\nearth, by [volume](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volume \"Volume\") of water.[4]\n\nThe Kaieteur National Park Act was enacted by the government to protect the\nregion's wealth of natural resources, its unique natural features (such as the\naforementioned Kaieteur Falls), its\n[biodiversity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biodiversity \"Biodiversity\") of\nAmazonian flora and fauna, and for the several\n[Amerindian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indigenous_peoples_of_the_Americas\n\"Indigenous peoples of the Americas\") tribes whom have lived and thrived in\nthis forest for millennia.[4] The Act is administered by the Kaieteur National\nPark Commission. There are organisms unique to this region that cannot be\nfound anywhere else on earth, such as the golden rocket frog (_[Anomaloglossus\nbeebei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anomaloglossus_beebei \"Anomaloglossus\nbeebei\")_), a diminutive [frog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frog \"Frog\")\nthat only breeds within the leaves of the\n[bromeliad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bromeliaceae \"Bromeliaceae\") species\n_[Brocchinia micrantha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brocchinia_micrantha\n\"Brocchinia micrantha\")_ (within the park), and nowhere else in the world.\n\nThe region is served by [Kaieteur International\nAirport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaieteur_International_Airport\n\"Kaieteur International Airport\"), which is situated at Kaieteur Falls.\n\n## Boundaries\n\n[[edit](/w/index.php?title=Kaieteur_National_Park&action=edit&section=1 \"Edit\nsection: Boundaries\")]\n\nIn 1999 the park's area was increased from 5 square miles (10 km2) to 242\nsquare miles (630 km2) by a Presidential Order.\n\n## Mining\n\n[[edit](/w/index.php?title=Kaieteur_National_Park&action=edit&section=2 \"Edit\nsection: Mining\")]\n\nIn 2017, two dozen villagers from the Chenapau community were arrested for\nillegal [mining](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mining \"Mining\") within the\nKaieteur National Park boundaries but were later released without any\ncharges.[5]\n\nIn November 2023, villagers were again accused of illegal mining within the\npark boundaries, which led to a protest by the villagers voicing their need to\nmine as a main source of income. The Chief Warden and the Guyana Geology and\nMines Commission (GGMC) ordered the protesters to leave or face consequences.\nThe villagers claimed to be mining in a [buffer\nzone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buffer_zone \"Buffer zone\") that was marked\nas land to be used for mining in accordance with the Amerindian Act.[5]\n\nMinister of Natural Resources Vickram Bharrat claimed that the GGMC will help\nthe villagers find another area to mine that does not include boundaries\nwithin the National Park.[5]\n\n## References\n\n[[edit](/w/index.php?title=Kaieteur_National_Park&action=edit&section=3 \"Edit\nsection: References\")]\n\n  1. \u2191 [_BBC Wildlife Magazine_](https://books.google.com/books?id=1JZFAQAAIAAJ).
