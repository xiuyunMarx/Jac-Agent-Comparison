[LIX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl_LIX_halftime_show \"Super Bowl LIX halftime show\")  \n(2025)  \n---|---|---  \n  \nThe **Super Bowl LVIII halftime show** , officially known as the **[Apple\nMusic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apple_Music \"Apple Music\") Super Bowl\nLVIII Halftime Show**, was the [halftime\nentertainment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halftime_show \"Halftime show\") of\n[Super Bowl LVIII](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl_LVIII \"Super Bowl\nLVIII\"), which took place on February 11, 2024, at [Allegiant\nStadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allegiant_Stadium \"Allegiant Stadium\")\nin [Paradise, Nevada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paradise,_Nevada\n\"Paradise, Nevada\").[1] It featured\n[Usher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Usher_\\(musician\\) \"Usher \\(musician\\)\")\nas the headline performer, with guest appearances by [Alicia\nKeys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alicia_Keys \"Alicia Keys\"),\n[will.i.am](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will.i.am \"Will.i.am\"), [Lil\nJon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lil_Jon \"Lil Jon\"),\n[Ludacris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludacris \"Ludacris\"),\n[H.E.R](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H.E.R. \"H.E.R.\"), and [Sonic Boom of the\nSouth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sonic_Boom_of_the_South \"Sonic Boom of\nthe South\").[2] The show was televised nationally by\n[CBS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NFL_on_CBS \"NFL on CBS\") and streamed on\n[Paramount+](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paramount+ \"Paramount+\") and the\nApple Music app.[3] The television broadcast was watched by 129 million\nviewers in the US, making it the third most watched halftime show in\nhistory.[4]\n\n## Background\n\n[[edit](/w/index.php?title=Super_Bowl_LVIII_halftime_show&action=edit&section=1\n\"Edit section: Background\")]\n\n### Artists in contention to headline the halftime show\n\n[[edit](/w/index.php?title=Super_Bowl_LVIII_halftime_show&action=edit&section=2\n\"Edit section: Artists in contention to headline the halftime show\")]\n\nThroughout summer 2023, there were various names being reported to be\nperforming at the halftime show, including former [One\nDirection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One_Direction \"One Direction\") member\nand British singer [Harry Styles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Styles\n\"Harry Styles\") to promote his recent album _[Harry's\nHouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry's_House \"Harry's House\")_ , which\nwon the [Album of the Year\nAward](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Album_of_the_Year\n\"Grammy Award for Album of the Year\") at the [2023 Grammy\nAwards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/65th_Annual_Grammy_Awards \"65th Annual\nGrammy Awards\").[5] [Taylor Swift](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_Swift\n\"Taylor Swift\") was reportedly considered for the show; she later pulled out\nof the running due to having prior commitments such as her [Eras\nTour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Eras_Tour \"The Eras Tour\"), which was\nreported by\n_[Cosmopolitan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cosmopolitan_\\(magazine\\)\n\"Cosmopolitan \\(magazine\\)\")_ in August 2023.[6] Still, Swift was in\nattendance,[7] leading to speculation that [her\npopularity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cultural_impact_of_Taylor_Swift\n\"Cultural impact of Taylor Swift\") helped cause the actual football game (and\nthe halftime show as a carryover) to become to the most-viewed television\nbroadcast in modern history. [Miley\nCyrus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miley_Cyrus \"Miley Cyrus\") was another\nartist also being considered to headline the show.[8] Other rumored artists\nincluded [Bad Bunny](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bad_Bunny \"Bad Bunny\"),\n[Jack Harlow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Harlow \"Jack Harlow\"), and\n[NSYNC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NSYNC \"NSYNC\").[9] Usher was informed of\nhis selection on August 11, 2023.\n\n### Headliner announcement\n\n[[edit](/w/index.php?title=Super_Bowl_LVIII_halftime_show&action=edit&section=3\n\"Edit section: Headliner announcement\")]\n\n[![](//thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/f/fa/Usher_Cannes_2016_retusche.jpg/250px-\nUsher_Cannes_2016_retusche.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Usher_Cannes_2016_retusche.jpg)[Usher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Usher_\\(musician\\)\n\"Usher \\(musician\\)\") headlined the halftime show as part of [Super Bowl\nLVIII](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl_LVIII \"Super Bowl LVIII\") on\nFebruary 11, 2024, two days after the release date of his ninth studio album\n_[Coming Home](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coming_Home_\\(Usher_album\\)\n\"Coming Home \\(Usher album\\)\")_.[10]\n\nOn September 24, 2023, it was announced that Usher would headline the show\nwith this being his second appearance at a halftime show following the [Super\nBowl XLV halftime\nshow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl_XLV_halftime_show \"Super Bowl\nXLV halftime show\") which was headlined by American musical group the [Black\nEyed Peas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Eyed_Peas \"Black Eyed Peas\")\nwhere he was a guest alongside\n[Slash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slash_\\(musician\\) \"Slash\n\\(musician\\)\"), making Raymond the first act to perform during both a Super\nBowl and an [NBA Finals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_NBA_Finals \"2015\nNBA Finals\"). He had been reported to be a potential headliner for a while\nafter extending his residency at the [MGM Grand Garden\nArena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MGM_Grand_Garden_Arena \"MGM Grand Garden\nArena\").[11] His participation is the latest result of the [National Football\nLeague](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Football_League \"National\nFootball League\") (NFL)'s partnership with [Jay\nZ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jay_Z \"Jay Z\")'s entertainment agency [Roc\nNation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roc_Nation \"Roc Nation\"), which was\nsigned in 2019 to boost the quality of the halftime shows. Coincidentally,\nUsher marked his thirtieth anniversary as a solo artist (1994\u20132024),[12] and\nthe lead-up to his headlining prominently made note of that fact. After his\nannouncement as the headliner, he said: \"It's a honor of a lifetime to finally\ncheck a Super Bowl performance off my bucket list. I can't wait to bring the\nworld a show unlike anything else they've seen from me before\". Speaking to\n[Apple Music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apple_Music \"Apple Music\")'s [Zane\nLowe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zane_Lowe \"Zane Lowe\") on potential\nguests, he said, \"Well, one thing I can say is that I have collaborated with a\nlot of incredible artists throughout the years,\" including fellow singers and\nrappers [Alicia Keys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alicia_Keys \"Alicia\nKeys\"), [will.i.am](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will.i.am \"Will.i.am\"), [Lil\nJon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lil_Jon \"Lil Jon\"),\n[Ludacris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludacris \"Ludacris\"), and\n[H.E.R.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H.E.R. \"H.E.R.\") playing guitar. As a\nnod to [Black History Month](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_History_Month\n\"Black History Month\"), the [HBCU](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HBCU \"HBCU\")\nmarching band [Sonic Boom of the\nSouth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sonic_Boom_of_the_South \"Sonic Boom of\nthe South\") played on-field brass and percussion.[10][13][14]\n\n### Alicia Keys' vocal gaffe\n\n[[edit](/w/index.php?title=Super_Bowl_LVIII_halftime_show&action=edit&section=4\n\"Edit section: Alicia Keys' vocal gaffe\")]\n\n[Alicia Keys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alicia_Keys \"Alicia Keys\")' voice\ncracked at the beginning of her performance of \"[If I Ain't Got\nYou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/If_I_Ain't_Got_You \"If I Ain't Got You\")\".\nIn the [YouTube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/YouTube \"YouTube\") upload of\nthe halftime show, the [NFL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NFL \"NFL\") scrubbed\nthe mistake[15] by editing in the correct notes from a prior rehearsal\nperformance to fix Keys' off-key and pitchy vocals.[16][17][18]\n\n## Reception\n\n[[edit](/w/index.php?title=Super_Bowl_LVIII_halftime_show&action=edit&section=5\n\"Edit section: Reception\")]\n\nIn a four-star review, Adrian Horton of _[The\nGuardian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Guardian \"The Guardian\")_\ndescribed the performance \"frenetic and daring\" while \"at times chaotic and\nuncharacteristically wobbly\".[19] Dominic Patten of _[Deadline\nHollywood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deadline_Hollywood \"Deadline\nHollywood\")_ wrote \"as tight as the tone and pacing of the performance was,\nUsher felt very 20 years ago and flat,\" compared to the previous two shows and\n[Andra Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andra_Day \"Andra Day\")'s \"[Lift\nEvery Voice and Sing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lift_Every_Voice_and_Sing\n\"Lift Every Voice and Sing\")\" performance.[20]\n\nThis was the 32nd edition of the Super Bowl Halftime Show, and topped out with\n129.3 million concurrent viewers, making it the biggest television audience in\nits history since the [inaugural\nshow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl_XXVII_halftime_show \"Super Bowl\nXXVII halftime show\") featuring [Michael\nJackson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Jackson \"Michael Jackson\")\n(133.4 million).[21]\n\nUsher's album, _[Coming\nHome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coming_Home_\\(Usher_album\\) \"Coming Home\n\\(Usher album\\)\")_ , was propelled to No. 2 on the Billboard 200 charts upon\nits debut the following week with 91,000\n[units](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Album-equivalent_unit \"Album-equivalent\nunit\") moved.[22] The record held the #1 slot on\n[iTunes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ITunes \"ITunes\") the week leading up\nthe big game.[23]\n\nThe show was nominated for [Outstanding Variety Special\n(Live)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Variety_Special_\\(Live\\)\n\"Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Variety Special \\(Live\\)\"), [Outstanding\nLighting Design / Lighting Direction for a Variety\nSpecial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Lighting_Design_/_Lighting_Direction_for_a_Variety_Special\n\"Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Lighting Design / Lighting Direction for\na Variety Special\") and [Outstanding Technical Direction and Camerawork for a\nSpecial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Technical_Direction_and_Camerawork_for_a_Special\n\"Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Technical Direction and Camerawork for a\nSpecial\") at the [76th Primetime Creative Arts Emmy\nAwards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/76th_Primetime_Creative_Arts_Emmy_Awards\n\"76th Primetime Creative Arts Emmy Awards\").\n\n## Set list\n\n[[edit](/w/index.php?title=Super_Bowl_LVIII_halftime_show&action=edit&section=6\n\"Edit section: Set list\")]\n\nSetlist adapted from
