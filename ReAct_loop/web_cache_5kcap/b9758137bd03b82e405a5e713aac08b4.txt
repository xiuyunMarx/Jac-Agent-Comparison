Trillions of dollars.\n[\n3\n]\nThe World's Billionaires\nis an annual ranking by the American business magazine\nForbes\nof people who are\nbillionaires\n, i.e., they are estimated to have a\nnet worth\nof US$1 billion or more. The list was first published in March 1987.\n[\n1\n]\nThe net worth of each individual is cited in\nUnited States dollars\n, based on their documented assets, and accounting for debt and other factors. This ranking excludes\nroyalty\nand\ndictators\nwhose wealth comes from their positions,\n[\n4\n]\nas well as people whose wealth is not able to be completely ascertained.\n[\n5\n]\nIn 2018,\nAmazon\nfounder\nJeff Bezos\nwas first ranked at the top and became the first\ncentibillionaire\nincluded in the ranking,\n[\n6\n]\nsurpassing\nMicrosoft\nfounder\nBill Gates\n, who had topped the list 18 of the previous 24 years.\n[\n7\n]\nIn 2022, Bezos was surpassed by\nElon Musk\n.\n[\n8\n]\nIn 2023, Musk was surpassed by French businessman\nBernard Arnault\n, the first French person to top the list.\n[\n9\n]\nIn 2025 and 2026, Musk once again topped the list.\n[\n10\n]\nMethodology\nEach year,\nForbes\nemploys a team of over 50 reporters from a variety of countries to track the activity of the world's wealthiest individuals\n[\n11\n]\nand sometimes groups or families \u2013 who share wealth. Preliminary surveys are sent to those who may qualify for the list. According to\nForbes\n, they received three types of responses\n\u2013 some people try to inflate their wealth, others cooperate but leave out details, and some refuse to answer any questions.\n[\n12\n]\nBusiness deals are then scrutinized and estimates of valuable assets\n\u2013 land, homes, vehicles, artwork, etc.\n\u2013 are made. Interviews are conducted to vet the figures and improve the estimate of an individual's holdings. Finally, positions in a publicly traded stock are priced to market on a date roughly a month before publication. Privately held companies are priced by the prevailing\nprice-to-sales\nor\nprice-to-earnings\nratios. Known debt is subtracted from assets to get a final estimate of an individual's estimated worth in United States dollars.
